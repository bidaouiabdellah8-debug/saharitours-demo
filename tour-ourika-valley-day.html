About This Tour

Just south of Marrakech, the road climbs gently into the High Atlas foothills and follows the Ourika River upstream through one of the most scenic valleys in the region. This shorter, relaxed day trip is ideal if you want a genuine taste of mountain Morocco without a long drive — Berber villages built into the hillside, a working argan oil cooperative run by local women, and a walk toward the cascades of Setti Fatma, all within a single easy half-day out of the city.

Half-Day Itinerary

09:00
Depart
Departure from Marrakech
Marrakech → Ourika Valley (≈35 km · ~1 hour)

Your driver collects you from your hotel or riad and heads south out of the city, quickly swapping the palm groves of the Marrakech plain for the first green folds of the High Atlas foothills.

10:00
Visit
Argan Oil Women's Cooperative
Roadside stop · 20–30 min · Free entry

A short stop at a women's argan oil cooperative on the way into the valley. Watch the traditional hand-cracking and grinding process used to press oil from the argan nut — a craft and income source unique to this part of Morocco — with the option to buy oil, cosmetics, or amlou directly from the cooperative.

Live demonstration — see the traditional grinding stones women use to crack and press argan nuts by hand
Supporting local women — the cooperative model provides direct income to Berber women in the region
10:45
Drive
Scenic Drive Through the Ourika Valley
Along the Ourika River · Photo stops en route

Continue along the river road as the valley narrows and climbs. Mud-brick Berber houses are terraced into the steep hillsides on either side, small orchards and vegetable plots line the riverbanks, and the peaks of the High Atlas rise ahead. We stop wherever the views are best for photos.

Terraced Berber villages — traditional mud-brick homes stacked into the hillsides above the river
Monday souk — if your visit falls on a Monday, we can time a stop at the weekly Ourika market where locals from surrounding villages come to trade
11:30
Arrive
Arrival in Setti Fatma
End of the paved road · Base for the waterfall walk

Setti Fatma is the last village at the head of the valley, and the starting point for the walk to the seven waterfalls the area is known for. Riverside cafés on wooden platforms over the water mark the start of the trail.

11:45
Hike
Walk Toward the Setti Fatma Waterfalls
Optional hike · ~45 min–1h round trip to the first cascade

Setti Fatma is famous for a series of seven cascades tumbling down the mountainside above the village. Most visitors walk to the first and most accessible waterfall — a rocky, occasionally steep path along the river — while more energetic hikers can continue further with a local guide. Purely optional; you're free to stay in the village instead.

The first waterfall — a refreshing cascade reached via a scenic riverside path lined with walnut trees
Seven cascades — for those who want to go further, local guides can lead you up to the higher falls
13:00
Lunch
Lunch Break — Riverside Café
Table over the Ourika River (lunch not included in price)

Enjoy lunch at one of the wooden terrace restaurants built directly over the river in Setti Fatma — a wonderfully cool, shaded spot on a warm day. Tagines, grilled trout, and mint tea are local specialties. Budget approximately $8–12 per person.

15:00
Return
Return Drive to Marrakech
Ourika Valley → Marrakech (≈35 km · ~1 hour) · Arrive ~17:00

Head back down the valley toward Marrakech, retracing the river road as the afternoon light softens over the foothills. Drop-off at your hotel or riad — end of tour, with the rest of your evening free.
