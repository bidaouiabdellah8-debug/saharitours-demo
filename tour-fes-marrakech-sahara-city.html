About This Tour

Journey across Morocco in four unforgettable days, from the ancient imperial city of Fes to the vibrant Red City of Marrakech. Cross the pine-clad slopes of the Middle Atlas, watch the sun set and rise over the golden dunes of the Sahara, wind through dramatic gorges and rose-scented valleys, and stand before the legendary kasbah of Ait Ben Haddou. Unlike a standard one-way transfer, this tour keeps an entire fourth day free for Marrakech itself — so instead of arriving late and rushing off, you get a full, unhurried day discovering the Red City's palaces, souks and monuments before your trip ends.

Short on time, or already have Marrakech covered? Our classic 4-Day Fes to Marrakech via Sahara tour follows the same desert route and arrives directly in Marrakech on Day 4 — no extra sightseeing day included.

4-Day Itinerary

Day 1
Fes → Merzouga
Fes → Ifrane → Azrou → Midelt → Ziz Valley → Merzouga
07:00
Depart
Early Morning Departure from Fes
Ifrane · 1,664m altitude · Alpine mountain town

Your adventure begins with an early morning pick-up from your hotel or riad in Fes. Head into the Middle Atlas Mountains and stop in Ifrane, admired for its alpine-style architecture, tidy streets and cool mountain scenery at 1,664m altitude.

09:30
Stop
Azrou Cedar Forest — Barbary Macaques
Near Azrou · Ancient cedar forest · Amazigh villages

Continue through Azrou and its cedar forests, with a possible stop to spot the famous wild Barbary macaques among centuries-old trees. Carry on through classic Middle Atlas mountain scenery and traditional Amazigh (Berber) villages.

13:00
Lunch
Lunch Break in Midelt

Stop for a well-earned lunch break in Midelt, the "Apple Capital of Morocco," nestled between the Middle and High Atlas ranges.

14:30
View
Ziz Valley Viewpoint
Panoramic viewpoint · Palm oasis · Pre-Saharan landscape

Stop at a spectacular viewpoint over the Ziz Valley, one of Morocco's largest palm oases, with thousands of date palms and traditional ksour villages stretching across the pre-Saharan landscape.

16:00
Pass
Erfoud & Rissani
Erfoud — fossils & marble · Rissani — historic caravan town

Pass through Erfoud, known throughout Morocco for its fossils and marble workshops, then Rissani, the historic caravan town and birthplace of the Alaouite Dynasty — with a stop at its traditional markets if time allows.

17:30
Arrive
Merzouga — Camel Ride to the Dunes & Desert Camp
Erg Chebbi dunes · Sunset camel trek · Night 1 in desert camp

Arrive in Merzouga in the late afternoon. In the evening, transfer by camel out into the dunes to watch the sunset paint the Sahara gold, then continue to your desert camp for a traditional dinner, live Berber music and stargazing under some of the darkest skies on Earth.

Camel transfer into Erg Chebbi at golden sunset — included in price
Desert camp — traditional Berber tent, Moroccan dinner & breakfast included
Day 2
Merzouga → Ouarzazate
Merzouga → Todra Gorges → Dades Valley → Skoura → Ouarzazate
06:00
Sunrise
Sunrise over the Dunes

Wake early to watch the sun rise over the Erg Chebbi dunes, the sand shifting from deep purple to amber gold. Enjoy breakfast at camp before heading back toward Merzouga to begin the day's drive.

08:30
Drive
Toward Tinghir via Rissani & Erfoud

Retrace the route through Rissani and Erfoud before turning toward Tinghir, watching the landscape shift from desert flats into the foothills of the Atlas.

11:00
Arrive
Todra Gorges — 300m Limestone Cliffs
Tinghir · 300m sheer cliffs · Todra Valley palm groves

Arrive at the towering Todra Gorges, where limestone cliffs rise over 300 metres above a crystal-clear river. Walk the canyon floor between the sheer rock walls, then pass back through the palm groves of the Todra Valley.

13:00
Lunch
Lunch near Tinghir

Stop for lunch in the Tinghir / Todra area before continuing the drive south-west toward the Dades Valley.

14:30
Drive
Dades Valley & the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs
Dramatic rock formations · Palm groves · Fortified kasbahs

Drive through the scenic Dades Valley, known for its folded rock formations and palm groves, then follow the famous Road of a Thousand Kasbahs, lined with fortified mud-brick palaces and ancient watchtowers.

16:00
Visit
Rose Valley (Kalaa M'Gouna) & Skoura Oasis
Kalaa M'Gouna rose fields · Skoura palm oasis & kasbahs

Pass through the Rose Valley around Kalaa M'Gouna, famous for its rose plantations and rosewater production, then continue into the lush Skoura Oasis, dotted with historic earthen kasbahs beneath thousands of palms.

18:00
Arrive
Arrival in Ouarzazate
"Hollywood of Africa" · Night 2 in hotel

Arrive in Ouarzazate in the early evening. Dinner and overnight stay in a comfortable hotel, ready for a day of kasbahs and mountains tomorrow.

Day 3
Ouarzazate → Marrakech
Ouarzazate → Ait Ben Haddou → High Atlas → Marrakech
08:00
Visit
Taourirt Kasbah & Ouarzazate — Morocco's "City of Cinema"
Former Glaoui residence · Major Moroccan film studios

After breakfast, explore Ouarzazate, often called Morocco's "city of cinema" for its major film studios. Visit the striking Taourirt Kasbah, a former stronghold of the Glaoui dynasty, before continuing your journey.

09:30
Visit
Ait Ben Haddou — UNESCO World Heritage Kasbah
UNESCO World Heritage · Iconic international film location

Visit the magnificent Ksar of Ait Ben Haddou, one of the most famous historic sites in southern Morocco, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and a filming location for numerous international movies and productions. Enjoy free time to explore the fortified earthen village above the Ounila River.

UNESCO heritage ksar — a living example of southern Moroccan earthen architecture
Panoramic viewpoint — climb to the summit granary for a breathtaking view over the ksar and valley
11:30
Optional
Telouet Kasbah (Optional, Depending on Time)
Former Glaoui palace · Ornate painted interiors

Time and road conditions permitting, make a short detour to Telouet, the crumbling former palace of the Glaoui pashas, with faded but still striking painted ceilings and carved plasterwork.

13:00
Drive
Tizi n'Tichka Pass — High Atlas Mountains
Altitude 2,260 m · Highest road pass in Morocco · Berber villages

Cross the spectacular High Atlas Mountains via the famous Tizi n'Tichka Pass at 2,260 metres — the highest paved road pass in Morocco. Stop along the way to admire mountain scenery and Berber villages clinging to the slopes.

17:00
Arrive
Arrival in Marrakech
Check-in at your hotel or riad · Free evening

Arrive in Marrakech in the early evening and check in to your hotel or riad. The evening is free to relax before a full day of Marrakech sightseeing tomorrow — unlike a one-way transfer tour, your trip isn't over yet.

Day 4
Marrakech Discovery
Full-Day Marrakech City Sightseeing Tour
09:00
Visit
Koutoubia Mosque
Marrakech's tallest minaret · Symbol of the Red City

Begin your Marrakech discovery day at the Koutoubia Mosque, the city's largest mosque and most recognizable landmark. Admire its elegant 12th-century minaret, the model for Seville's Giralda and Rabat's Hassan Tower, from the surrounding gardens.

09:45
Visit
Jemaa el-Fna Square
UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage · Heart of the Medina

Walk through the legendary Jemaa el-Fna Square, a UNESCO-recognised masterpiece of oral and intangible heritage, alive by day with orange-juice stalls, snake charmers and henna artists.

10:30
Explore
Marrakech Medina & the Souks
Labyrinth of souks · Spices, leather, lamps & textiles

Wander the maze-like alleys of the old Medina and its souks, where century-old trades continue side by side — spice merchants, leather tanners, lantern makers and weavers of colourful textiles.

12:00
Visit
Bahia Palace
19th-century vizier's palace · Carved cedar & zellige tilework

Visit the opulent Bahia Palace, a 19th-century masterpiece built for the Grand Vizier, with courtyards, painted cedar ceilings and intricate zellige tilework showcasing the height of Moroccan craftsmanship.

13:00
Lunch
Lunch Break in the Medina

Take a lunch break at a local restaurant in the Medina before continuing the afternoon's sightseeing.

14:00
Visit
Saadian Tombs
16th-century royal necropolis · Rediscovered in 1917

Discover the Saadian Tombs, the beautifully restored royal necropolis of the Saadian dynasty, sealed off for centuries and rediscovered only in 1917, with dazzling marble and carved stucco chambers.

15:00
Visit
El Badi Palace
Ruined 16th-century royal palace · Panoramic ramparts

Walk the sunken orange gardens and vast courtyards of El Badi Palace, once "the Incomparable" palace of Sultan Ahmad al-Mansur, now an evocative ruin with sweeping views from its ramparts.

16:00
Optional
Majorelle Garden & Menara Gardens (Optional)
Cobalt-blue botanical garden · Olive-grove reflecting pool

If time and energy allow, round off the day with an optional visit to the vivid cobalt-blue Majorelle Garden, once owned by Yves Saint Laurent, and the tranquil Menara Gardens with their olive groves and reflecting pool below the Atlas peaks.

18:00
End
End of Tour — Marrakech Drop-off
Drop-off at your hotel or riad, or airport transfer on request

In the evening, your driver drops you off at your hotel or riad in Marrakech (or transfers you to the airport if that's your onward plan). Your four-day journey across Morocco comes to an end — Middle Atlas forests, Sahara dunes, mountain passes, ancient kasbahs, and a full day discovering Marrakech itself.
