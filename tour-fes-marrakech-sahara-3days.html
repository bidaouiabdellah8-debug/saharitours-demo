About This Tour

This is the fast, efficient way to cross Morocco from Fes to Marrakech via the Sahara — the same headline highlights as our classic route, condensed into 3 days and 2 nights instead of 4. You still get the Middle Atlas, a sunset camel trek into the Erg Chebbi dunes, a night under the stars in a traditional desert camp, the towering Todra Gorge, the Dades Valley, Ait Ben Haddou, and the High Atlas — just with one night in the desert instead of two, and a faster overall pace. Ideal for travelers on a tighter schedule or a tighter budget who don't want to miss a single highlight.

Got an extra day to spare? Our classic 4-Day Fes to Marrakech via Sahara tour adds a full second day exploring the Merzouga region, or go further with the 4-Day Sahara + Marrakech City Day version, which tacks on a full day of sightseeing in Marrakech itself.

3-Day Express Itinerary

Day 1
Fes → Merzouga
Fes → Ifrane → Midelt → Ziz Valley → Merzouga
07:00
Depart
Early Morning Pick-up from Fes

Your express Sahara adventure begins with an early pick-up from your hotel or riad in Fes. Head into the Middle Atlas Mountains and stop in Ifrane, known as the "Switzerland of Morocco" for its alpine architecture and pine-covered hills at 1,664m altitude.

09:30
Stop
Cedar Forest — Barbary Macaques
Near Azrou · Ancient cedar ecosystem · Endemic wildlife

Continue to the cedar forests near Azrou, home to Morocco's wild Barbary macaques. Look for these free-roaming primates among trees that are centuries old, then press on south — today's schedule is quick-paced by design.

13:00
Lunch
Midelt & the Ziz Valley

Stop for lunch in Midelt, the "Apple Capital of Morocco," then continue through the dramatic Ziz Valley and gorge — one of Morocco's largest palm oases, lined with thousands of date palms and traditional ksour villages carved from red earth.

17:00
Arrive
Merzouga — Camel Ride & Desert Camp
Erg Chebbi dunes · Camel trek at sunset · Night in desert camp

Arrive in Merzouga in the late afternoon. Mount your camel for a trek deep into the Erg Chebbi dunes at golden sunset, then reach your traditional desert camp for a Berber dinner, live music around the campfire, and stargazing under one of the world's darkest skies.

Camel trekking into Erg Chebbi at golden sunset — included in price
Desert camp — traditional Berber tent, Moroccan dinner, live music around the campfire
Day 2
Merzouga → Ouarzazate
Merzouga → Todra Gorge → Dades Valley → Ouarzazate
06:00
Sunrise
Sahara Sunrise & Departure from Camp

Rise early to watch the dunes glow gold as the sun climbs over Erg Chebbi. After breakfast at camp, head straight back out of the desert — the express itinerary is built for pace, covering three major sights in a single day.

08:00
Depart
Rissani & the Road to Todra
Rissani caravan town · Tinghir · En route to Todra Gorge

Drive out via Rissani, the historic caravan town and birthplace of the Alaouite Dynasty, then continue toward Tinghir at the gateway to the Todra Gorge.

10:30
Arrive
Todra Gorge — 300m Limestone Cliffs
Tinghir · 300m sheer cliffs · Crystal-clear Todra River

Arrive at the Todra Gorge, where towering limestone cliffs rise more than 300 metres above a crystal-clear river cutting through the rock. Take a short, refreshing walk into the canyon floor for photos of one of Morocco's most dramatic landscapes before continuing.

The gorge floor walk — the narrowest point is just a few metres wide, with sheer 300m walls rising on both sides
Todra River — a ribbon of icy blue water flowing at the base of the canyon, lined with palm trees and poplar groves
13:00
Lunch
Dades Valley — Kasbahs & Red Rock
Dramatic rock formations · Palm groves · Berber villages

Stop for lunch, then drive through the scenic Dades Valley, known for its dramatic pink-and-ochre rock formations, palm groves, and traditional Berber villages perched on the hillsides. In the express version this is folded straight into today's drive rather than an overnight stop, keeping the pace fast.

16:30
Arrive
Ouarzazate — Atlas Film Studios
"Hollywood of Africa" · Atlas Film Studios · Overnight in Ouarzazate

Arrive in Ouarzazate, often called the "Hollywood of Africa," with a stop at the famous Atlas Film Studios — sets and props from Gladiator, Game of Thrones, The Mummy and more. Dinner and overnight in Ouarzazate itself — a faster pace than the classic 4-day tour, which overnights back in the Dades Valley.

Atlas Film Studios — one of the world's largest film studios, sets from Hollywood blockbusters
Overnight in Ouarzazate — puts you closer to Ait Ben Haddou for an earlier, more relaxed final day
Day 3
Ouarzazate → Marrakech
Ouarzazate → Ait Ben Haddou → High Atlas → Marrakech
08:30
Visit
Ait Ben Haddou — UNESCO World Heritage Site
UNESCO World Heritage · Gladiator & Game of Thrones · Free time to explore

After breakfast, visit the magnificent Kasbah of Ait Ben Haddou — a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of Morocco's most iconic landmarks. This fortified ksar of earthen clay architecture rising above the Ounila River has appeared in dozens of international films and TV series including Gladiator and Game of Thrones. Enjoy free time to explore the ancient fortified village.

UNESCO heritage ksar — a living example of southern Moroccan earthen architecture, inhabited for thousands of years
Panoramic viewpoint — climb to the summit granary for a breathtaking view over the entire ksar and valley
11:00
Drive
Tizi n'Tichka Pass — High Atlas Mountains
Altitude 2,260 m · Highest road pass in Morocco · Berber mountain villages

Cross the spectacular High Atlas Mountains via the famous Tizi n'Tichka Pass at 2,260 metres — the highest paved road pass in Morocco. Stop along the way to admire breathtaking mountain scenery and ancient Berber villages clinging to the slopes.

15:30
Arrive
Arrival in Marrakech — End of Tour
Drop-off at your hotel or riad in Marrakech

Arrive in Marrakech in the late afternoon or early evening, where your driver will drop you off at your hotel or riad. Three days, one desert night, and every headline highlight of the Sahara crossing — the fast way to see it all.
