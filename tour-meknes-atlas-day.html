About This Tour

Just an hour from Meknes, the Middle Atlas is a different Morocco entirely — cool pine and cedar forests, alpine-style villages, and mountain air that earned Ifrane its nickname, "the Switzerland of Morocco." This easy full-day trip visits Ifrane's chalets and stone lion statue, the bustling market town of Azrou, and the ancient Cedar Forest where wild Barbary macaques roam freely among trees centuries old, before a scenic drive back through Amazigh (Berber) villages to Meknes.

Full-Day Itinerary

08:00
Depart
Departure from Meknes
Meknes → Ifrane (≈65 km · ~1 hour)

Morning pick-up from your hotel in Meknes for a short, easy drive up into the Middle Atlas, with the temperature dropping and the scenery turning green as you climb.

09:00
Visit
Ifrane, the "Switzerland of Morocco"
European-style chalets · Stone Lion Statue · ~45 minutes

Stroll through Ifrane's peaked red roofs and alpine chalets — so unlike anywhere else in Morocco that it has earned the nickname "the Switzerland of Morocco." Stop for photos at the famous carved stone lion statue in the town centre, a local landmark since the 1930s.

Alpine chalets — red-tiled, peaked-roof architecture unique in Morocco, built during the French protectorate era
Stone Lion Statue — Ifrane's beloved carved landmark, a favourite photo stop right in the town centre
10:30
Visit
Azrou Market Town
Local crafts market · Cedar woodwork · ~30 minutes

Continue to Azrou, a lively Amazigh (Berber) market town built around a distinctive volcanic rock outcrop. Browse stalls of cedar-wood carvings, wool rugs, and local crafts among a working town with far fewer tourists than Ifrane.

11:30
Explore
Cedar Forest & Barbary Macaques
Ancient cedar trees · Wild Barbary macaques · ~1 hour

Drive into the heart of the Cedar Forest, home to some of the oldest and tallest Atlas cedars in Morocco. Keep your eyes open along the roadside for troops of wild Barbary macaques, one of the last strongholds of this endangered species — a memorable, safe wildlife encounter right from your vehicle.

13:00
Lunch
Lunch Break
Local restaurant near Ifrane or Azrou · Trout specialty (lunch not included)

Enjoy lunch at a local restaurant in the Ifrane or Azrou area — a great spot to try fresh river trout, a Middle Atlas specialty rarely found elsewhere in Morocco.

14:30
Drive
Middle Atlas Scenic Drive
Amazigh villages · Mountain viewpoints · ~1.5 hours

A relaxed scenic drive through rolling Middle Atlas mountain scenery, passing small Amazigh (Berber) villages, grazing sheep, and sweeping viewpoints over forested valleys on the way back toward Meknes.

16:30
Return
Return to Meknes
Middle Atlas → Meknes · Arrive ~18:00

Descend from the mountains back to Meknes, arriving at your hotel by early evening — end of tour.
