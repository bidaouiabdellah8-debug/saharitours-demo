About This Tour

Marrakech — Morocco's famous "Red City" — is a full day's round trip from Casablanca, but one that rewards the distance many times over. This tour gives you a genuine full day in the city: the theatre of Jemaa el-Fnaa square by daylight and again at dusk when the food stalls and performers take over, the towering Koutoubia Mosque, a photo stop at the exterior of the exotic Majorelle Garden, and hours of free time to lose yourself in the covered souks.

🎭 Jemaa el-Fnaa Square 🕌 Koutoubia Mosque 🌿 Majorelle Garden 🛍️ Covered Souks 🏛️ Optional Bahia Palace

Full-Day Itinerary

07:00
Depart
Early Departure from Casablanca
Casablanca → Marrakech via the highway (≈240 km · ~3 hours)

Your driver collects you from your hotel in Casablanca at 07:00 sharp for the drive south. Note: this tour departs from Casablanca, not Fes — please confirm your Casablanca pick-up address when booking. The journey runs mostly on the highway, with the landscape gradually turning drier and more open as you approach Marrakech.

10:00
Arrive
Koutoubia Mosque
Exterior & minaret view (non-Muslims cannot enter)

Arriving in Marrakech, we first pass the Koutoubia Mosque, whose 12th-century minaret is the tallest structure in the city and the model for several famous towers across the Muslim world, including the Giralda in Seville. As with all active mosques in Morocco, only the exterior and gardens are open to non-Muslim visitors, but it remains one of the most photographed landmarks in the city.

10:45
Photo
Majorelle Garden — Photo Stop
Exterior stop · Entry ticket optional & not included

A stop at the entrance of the Majorelle Garden, the vivid cobalt-blue villa and botanical garden created by French painter Jacques Majorelle and later restored by Yves Saint Laurent. If you'd like to go inside to see the cactus garden and Berber Museum, entry tickets can be purchased on-site (not included in the tour price and subject to availability).

12:00
Explore
The Souks of Marrakech
Covered market alleys · Leather, lamps, textiles, spices

Wander the maze of covered souks north of Jemaa el-Fnaa — a dense network of alleys where entire streets specialize in leather goods, hammered brass lanterns, wool and silk textiles, and mounds of spices. Your driver-guide can point you toward the more authentic artisan workshops away from the busiest tourist stalls.

13:30
Lunch
Lunch Break in the Medina
Restaurant in the medina (lunch not included in price)

A break for lunch at a local restaurant in the medina — tagines, couscous, and grilled meats are the local staples. Budget approximately $10–15 per person.

15:00
Optional
Bahia Palace (Optional)
19th-century palace · Entry ticket optional & not included

For those interested, we can include a visit to the 19th-century Bahia Palace, famous for its carved cedar ceilings, painted stucco, and tranquil courtyards — a fine example of Moroccan palace architecture. Entry ticket purchased on-site if you choose to visit.

17:00
Free
Jemaa el-Fnaa Square — Day into Dusk
~2.5 hours free · UNESCO-listed square · Best at dusk

Free time in and around Jemaa el-Fnaa, the great square at the heart of the medina and a UNESCO-recognized "Masterpiece of the Oral and Intangible Heritage of Humanity." By day, watch snake charmers, henna artists, and orange juice stalls; as the sun sets, dozens of food stalls light up and storytellers, musicians, and performers take over the square for its famous evening atmosphere.

Storytellers & snake charmers — living traditions performed daily in the square
Evening food stalls — the square transforms at dusk into an open-air night market of grilled food and juice stands
19:30
Return
Return to Casablanca
Marrakech → Casablanca via the highway (≈240 km · ~3 hours) · Arrive ~21:00

Depart Marrakech as the evening settles in and drive back to Casablanca on the highway. Arrive around 21:00 pm. Drop-off at your hotel or an agreed point in the city — end of tour.
