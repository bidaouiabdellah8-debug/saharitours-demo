About This Tour

Short on time but don't want to miss the Sahara? Our 2-day express tour is a compact version of our classic desert route, built for travellers who want the golden dunes of Erg Chebbi without giving up several days of their trip. You'll cross the same Middle Atlas Mountains, cedar forests, and Ziz Valley oasis as our longer tours, ride a camel into the dunes at sunset, and spend one unforgettable night under the stars in a Berber desert camp — then head straight back to Fes the next morning. It's the same magic, condensed into a fast, budget-friendly getaway.

Looking for a longer trip instead? See our classic 3-day version with an extra night in the desert and a stop at the Rissani market, or continue all the way to Marrakech with our 4-day Fes-to-Marrakech tour via Todra Gorge and Ait Ben Haddou.

2-Day Itinerary

Day 1
Fes → Merzouga
Fes → Ifrane → Cedar Forest → Midelt → Ziz Valley → Merzouga
07:00
Depart
Early Morning Pick-up from Fes
Fes → Middle Atlas Mountains (approx. 1h)

We collect you from your hotel or riad in Fes bright and early. Settle into your air-conditioned vehicle as we climb into the Middle Atlas Mountains, with the morning light gradually revealing the green hillsides ahead.

08:30
Arrive
Ifrane — "Switzerland of Morocco"
Altitude 1,664 m · Alpine architecture · Quick photo stop

A brief stop in Ifrane, nicknamed the "Switzerland of Morocco" for its tidy streets and alpine-style rooftops. Enough time to stretch your legs, snap a photo by the famous stone lion, and breathe the cool mountain air before continuing south.

09:45
Drive
Cedar Forest near Azrou — Wild Barbary Macaques
Near Azrou · Ancient cedars · Endemic wildlife

A short stop in the Cedar Forest near Azrou, where troops of wild Barbary macaques often gather beside the road — Morocco's only wild primates, completely at ease around visitors. The forest itself is centuries old, with some cedars said to be over 800 years old.

Wild Barbary macaques — Africa's only wild primates north of the Sahara, often seen right beside the road
13:00
Lunch
Lunch in Midelt — "Apple Capital of Morocco"
Midelt · Transition zone between Middle and High Atlas · Local restaurant

Lunch stop in Midelt, known locally for its apple orchards. From here the scenery changes fast — green mountains give way to rust-red plateaus as we head deeper toward the pre-Saharan south.

15:00
Drive
Ziz Valley — Palm Oasis
Ziz River Valley · Thousands of date palms · Traditional ksour villages

We wind through the Ziz Valley, a ribbon of thousands of palm trees threading between red-earth villages. A quick stop at the Tunnel du Légionnaire viewpoint gives you one of the best panoramas over the oasis in all of Morocco.

Tunnel du Légionnaire viewpoint — a sweeping panorama over the Ziz palm oasis
17:30
Arrive
Merzouga — Camel Ride at Sunset & Desert Camp
Erg Chebbi dunes · Up to 150 m high · Camel ride to desert camp

Arrive in Merzouga as the light turns gold. Your camel is waiting to carry you into the Erg Chebbi dunes for one of Morocco's most beautiful sunsets — the sand shifting from amber to deep orange as the sun drops below the horizon. Dinner, live Berber music, and a blanket of stars await at the desert camp.

Camel ride into Erg Chebbi — into the golden dunes at the magic hour before sunset
Berber desert camp — private tent, Moroccan dinner, live music around the campfire
Stargazing in the Sahara — no light pollution, millions of stars overhead
Day 2
Merzouga → Fes
Sunrise → Ziz Valley → Midelt → Ifrane → Fes
06:00
Sunrise
Sahara Sunrise over Erg Chebbi
Dune summit · Golden hour · Desert stillness

An early wake-up call for the sunrise over Erg Chebbi — climb a nearby dune crest to watch the sky shift through gold, pink, and orange as the first light hits the sand. A short but unforgettable moment before breakfast and the drive north.

08:00
Depart
Breakfast & Direct Return to Fes
Merzouga → Ziz Valley → Midelt → Ifrane → Fes

After breakfast at camp, we head directly back toward Fes, retracing the scenic route through the Ziz Valley, Midelt, and Ifrane. Because this express version skips the Rissani market and second desert night, you'll be back in Fes the same day — ideal if your schedule is tight.

13:00
Lunch
Lunch in Midelt

A stop for lunch in Midelt before crossing back over the Middle Atlas Mountains on the final leg north.

15:30
Stop
Short Break in Ifrane

A short break in Ifrane to stretch your legs in the mountain air before the final drive back to Fes.

17:30
Arrive
Arrive in Fes — End of Tour
Drop-off at your hotel or riad in Fes

Arrive back in Fes in the late afternoon, where your driver drops you at your hotel or riad. Two days, one unforgettable night in the Sahara — proof that you don't need a week to experience the desert.
