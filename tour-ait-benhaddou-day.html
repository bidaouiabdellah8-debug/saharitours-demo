About This Tour

This is Morocco's classic High Atlas crossing in a single long day — a dramatic drive over the Tizi n'Tichka pass, one of the country's highest paved mountain roads, followed by a visit to Ait Benhaddou, the extraordinary fortified clay city that has served as a backdrop for countless Hollywood productions. From there we continue to Ouarzazate, known as "the door of the desert," home to a historic kasbah and the sprawling Atlas film studios. It's a long day of driving, but the scenery and the history make every kilometer worthwhile.

Full-Day Itinerary

07:00
Depart
Early Departure from Marrakech
Marrakech → High Atlas Mountains (early start for a long day)

Given the distance covered today, we set off early. Your driver picks you up from your hotel or riad in Marrakech and we head south out of the city, watching the fertile Haouz plain give way to the foothills of the High Atlas.

09:00
Cross
Tizi n'Tichka Mountain Pass
Elevation 2,260m · One of Morocco's highest paved roads · Photo stops

The road climbs steeply into the High Atlas via a spectacular series of switchbacks, reaching the Tizi n'Tichka pass at 2,260 meters — one of the highest paved mountain roads in Morocco. We'll stop at scenic overlooks along the way to take in the dramatic views over terraced Berber villages, kasbah ruins clinging to the slopes, and layered mountain ridges stretching to the horizon.

Hairpin mountain road — one of Morocco's most dramatic drives, with switchbacks carved into the mountainside
Roadside mineral & craft stalls — local vendors selling amethyst, quartz and handmade crafts at the summit
11:30
Arrive
Ait Benhaddou — The UNESCO Ksar
Fortified earthen ksar · UNESCO World Heritage Site · Gladiator, Game of Thrones, Lawrence of Arabia

Arrive at Ait Benhaddou, a fortified ksar of tightly clustered earthen clay houses rising from the riverbank, protected within defensive towers. Cross the river (by footbridge or, in high water, by mule) and climb through the narrow lanes of this UNESCO World Heritage Site, famous as a filming location for Gladiator, Game of Thrones, Lawrence of Arabia, and many other productions. From the ruined agadir (fortified granary) at the top, a sweeping view opens over the valley below.

Fortified clay architecture — earthen towers and ramparts largely unchanged for centuries
Famous film location — sets and backdrops used in Gladiator, Game of Thrones, and Lawrence of Arabia
Hilltop agadir viewpoint — climb to the ruined granary at the summit for panoramic valley views
13:30
Lunch
Lunch Break — Views over the Ksar
Restaurant overlooking Ait Benhaddou (lunch not included in price)

Stop for lunch at a restaurant with a terrace overlooking the ksar and the river valley — a great spot to rest before continuing to Ouarzazate. Budget approximately $10–15 per person.

14:30
Visit
Ouarzazate — Door of the Desert
Taourirt Kasbah · Atlas Film Studios · "Door of the Desert"

Continue a short distance to Ouarzazate, nicknamed "the door of the desert" for its position at the gateway to the Sahara. Visit the Taourirt Kasbah, once the residence of the powerful Glaoui family, with its maze of clay-walled rooms and courtyards. Ouarzazate is also home to the Atlas film studios, among the largest in the world, where many of the same epic productions filmed at Ait Benhaddou built additional sets — you'll pass by the studio grounds on the way through town.

Taourirt Kasbah — former residence of the Glaoui family, with ornate clay-carved interiors
Atlas Film Studios — one of the world's largest film studio complexes, passed en route through town
16:00
Return
Return to Marrakech via Tizi n'Tichka
Ouarzazate → Marrakech via the High Atlas · Arrive ~21:00

Begin the long drive back over the Tizi n'Tichka pass to Marrakech, watching the mountain light change as the afternoon fades into evening. Arrive in Marrakech around 21:00 pm. Drop-off at your hotel or riad — end of tour.
