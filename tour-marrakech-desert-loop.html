About This Tour

Leaving from Marrakech, this loop climbs over the High Atlas Mountains via the legendary Tizi n'Tichka pass, visits the UNESCO-listed kasbah of Ait Ben Haddou, crosses the film-set town of Ouarzazate, and follows the Dades Valley toward the golden dunes of Erg Chebbi — where a camel caravan carries you into the Sahara for sunset and a night under the stars. Choose the 3-day express version for a faster taste of the desert, or add a fourth day to also walk the towering Todra Gorge and enjoy a second night near the dunes before the drive back to Marrakech.

✓ Tizi n'Tichka High Atlas Pass (2,260 m)
✓ Ait Ben Haddou — UNESCO World Heritage ksar
✓ Ouarzazate — the "Hollywood of Africa"
✓ Dades Valley rock formations
✓ Todra Gorge — 300m cliffs (4-day only)
✓ Camel ride across Erg Chebbi dunes
✓ Sahara sunset & sunrise
✓ Round-trip — back to your Marrakech hotel

Day-by-Day Itinerary

The itinerary below covers the full 4-day loop. If you book the 3-day option, your tour follows the same Days 1 and 2, then returns directly to Marrakech on Day 3 — see the note on Day 3 below.

Day 1
Marrakech → Dades Valley · Both options
Marrakech → Tizi n'Tichka → Ait Ben Haddou → Ouarzazate → Dades Valley
07:00
Depart
Early Departure from Marrakech
Marrakech → Tizi n'Tichka Pass (approx. 2.5h)

Your driver picks you up from your riad or hotel in Marrakech and heads south into the High Atlas Mountains. The road climbs steadily through Berber villages and terraced valleys toward one of Morocco's most famous mountain passes.

09:30
Stop
Tizi n'Tichka Pass — High Atlas Mountains
Altitude 2,260 m · Highest paved road pass in Morocco

A photo stop at the summit of the Tizi n'Tichka pass, the highest paved road in Morocco. Berber villages cling to the slopes below, and on clear days the view stretches for miles across the High Atlas range.

Summit viewpoint — panoramic views over terraced Berber villages and the High Atlas range
11:30
Visit
Ait Ben Haddou — UNESCO World Heritage Site
UNESCO ksar · Featured in Gladiator & Game of Thrones

Explore the fortified earthen ksar of Ait Ben Haddou, rising above the Ounila River. This UNESCO World Heritage Site has been the backdrop for dozens of films and TV series. Climb to the summit granary for a sweeping view over the valley below.

UNESCO heritage ksar — earthen clay architecture inhabited for centuries
Panoramic granary viewpoint — climb to the top for the classic postcard view
13:30
Lunch
Lunch in Ouarzazate — "Hollywood of Africa"
Major film studios · Kasbah town

Lunch stop in Ouarzazate, Morocco's biggest film production hub, where Gladiator, Game of Thrones, and Lawrence of Arabia were all shot. Time permitting, admire the Taourirt Kasbah before continuing east.

17:00
Arrive
Dades Valley — Night 1
Dramatic rock formations · Palm groves · Overnight in a valley hotel

Continue through the Dades Valley, known for its ochre rock formations, palm groves, and Berber villages perched on the hillside. Dinner and overnight at a hotel overlooking the valley.

Day 2
Dades Valley → Merzouga · Both options
Dades Valley → Todra Gorge (4-day) → Merzouga · Camel Ride & Desert Camp
08:00
Depart
Depart Dades Valley

After breakfast, we set off toward Merzouga. Travellers on the 4-day loop make a detour to Todra Gorge first; the 3-day loop continues more directly, with time for photo stops along the Route of a Thousand Kasbahs.

09:30
4-Day
Todra Gorge — 300m Limestone Cliffs 4-Day option only
Tinghir · 300m sheer cliffs · Crystal-clear Todra River

On the 4-day loop, we detour to Todra Gorge, where limestone cliffs rise over 300 metres above a narrow river canyon — one of Morocco's most dramatic natural sights. Free time to walk the canyon floor and take photos before continuing toward the dunes.

Gorge floor walk — sheer 300m walls rising above a cool river canyon
17:00
Arrive
Merzouga — Camel Ride at Sunset & Desert Camp
Erg Chebbi dunes · Up to 150m high · Night in a desert camp

Arrive in Merzouga in the late afternoon. Meet your camel caravan and ride into the Erg Chebbi dunes as the sun sets, painting the sand gold and orange. Dinner, live Berber music, and stargazing await at the desert camp.

Camel ride into Erg Chebbi — golden dunes at sunset
Berber desert camp — private tent, dinner, music around the campfire
Day 3
3-Day: Return to Marrakech · 4-Day: Explore & 2nd Desert Night
Sunrise over Erg Chebbi — then the two options diverge
06:00
Sunrise
Sahara Sunrise over Erg Chebbi

Wake up early to watch the sunrise over the dunes — a slow wave of gold and pink light spreading over Erg Chebbi. Breakfast at camp follows before the two itineraries diverge.

08:00
3-Day
3-Day Option: Direct Return to Marrakech Tour ends today
Merzouga → Ouarzazate → Tizi n'Tichka → Marrakech

If you booked the 3-day loop, after breakfast we drive the same scenic route back — Ouarzazate, then over the Tizi n'Tichka pass — arriving in Marrakech in the evening, where your driver drops you at your hotel. End of the 3-day tour.

09:00
4-Day
4-Day Option: Explore Merzouga & Second Desert Night 4-Day option only
Khamlia village · Nomadic families · Second night at the desert camp

If you booked the 4-day loop, this extra day is yours to enjoy the Sahara at a slower pace. Visit Khamlia village for its traditional Gnawa music, meet nomadic families living on the edge of the dunes, and relax before a second night at the desert camp.

Khamlia Gnawa music — ancestral rhythms unique to the deep south of Morocco
Optional ATV / buggy rides — dune adventures at extra cost
Day 4
4-Day option only · Merzouga → Marrakech
Merzouga → Ouarzazate → Ait Ben Haddou → Tizi n'Tichka → Marrakech
08:00
Depart
Return Journey to Marrakech
Retrace the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs · Ouarzazate · Tizi n'Tichka

After breakfast, begin the final drive back to Marrakech, retracing the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs through Ouarzazate and back over the Tizi n'Tichka pass. A last chance to catch photos of Ait Ben Haddou or the High Atlas villages if you missed anything on the way out.

17:00
Arrive
Arrival in Marrakech — End of Tour
Drop-off at your hotel or riad in Marrakech

Arrive back in Marrakech in the late afternoon, where your driver drops you at your hotel or riad — bringing your 4-day loop through kasbahs, gorges, and golden dunes to a close.
