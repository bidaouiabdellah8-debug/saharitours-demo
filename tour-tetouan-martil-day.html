About This Tour

This tour departs from Tangier, not Fes, and explores the stretch of Mediterranean coast just to its east. Tetouan's medina is a UNESCO World Heritage Site with a character all its own — shaped by generations of Andalusian refugees, its whitewashed walls, low doorways and wrought-iron balconies feel distinctly different from the medinas of Fes or Marrakech. From there we head down to the coast, to the long sandy beach of Martil and the working fishing port and marina of M'diq, for a day that mixes history with sea air.

Full-Day Itinerary

09:00
Depart
Departure from Tangier
Tangier → Tetouan (≈60 km · ~1 hour)

Your driver picks you up from your hotel or riad in Tangier at 09:00 and we head east along the coast road toward Tetouan, with the Rif foothills rising on one side and glimpses of the Mediterranean on the other.

10:00
Visit
Tetouan — The UNESCO Whitewashed Medina
UNESCO World Heritage medina (since 1997) · ~2 hours guided walk

Enter Tetouan's old medina, inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1997. Its architecture is unmistakably different from Morocco's other imperial cities — shaped by waves of Muslim and Jewish refugees from Al-Andalus, the streets are lined with whitewashed walls, ornate ironwork balconies, and Spanish colonial touches from the town's decades as capital of the Spanish protectorate zone. Walk through the souks, past craft workshops, and the leather and textile quarters.

Whitewashed streets — bright, sun-bleached walls quite unlike the earth tones of Fes or Marrakech
Andalusian influence — architecture and craftsmanship shaped by generations of refugees from Spain
Artisan souks — leatherwork, embroidery, and traditional crafts still made by hand in small workshops
12:15
Lunch
Lunch Break in Tetouan
Restaurant near the medina (lunch not included in price)

A relaxed lunch stop near the medina before we head to the coast. Budget approximately $10–15 per person.

13:15
Visit
Martil — Long Sandy Beach
Tetouan → Martil (≈10 km · ~15 minutes) · Free time on the beach

A short drive brings you down to Martil, Tetouan's seaside outlet on the Mediterranean, known for its long stretch of sandy beach and easygoing corniche promenade lined with cafés. Time to walk the beachfront, dip your feet in the water, or simply relax with a coffee facing the sea.

Long sandy beach — one of the most popular stretches of coastline in northern Morocco
Seafront corniche — a relaxed promenade of cafés and restaurants facing the Mediterranean
15:00
Visit
M'diq — Fishing Port & Marina
Martil → M'diq (≈8 km · ~15 minutes) · Working fishing port & marina

Continue along the coast to M'diq, a former fishing village that has grown into a popular small resort town while keeping its working port. Walk the marina among the colourful fishing boats and pleasure yachts, and enjoy views back across the bay toward Tetouan and the Rif Mountains.

Working fishing port — colourful boats and the daily catch, a glimpse of local life on the coast
Marina & bay views — pleasure boats moored alongside fishing vessels, with the bay stretching toward Tetouan
16:30
Return
Return to Tangier
M'diq → Tangier (≈55 km · ~1 hour) · Arrive ~19:00

We drive back along the coast to Tangier, arriving at your hotel around 19:00. Drop-off at your hotel or riad — end of tour.
