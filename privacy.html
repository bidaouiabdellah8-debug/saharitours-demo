Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

Saharitours ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us when you visit this website, request a quote, fill in our contact form, or book a tour. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can control it.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Contact & booking form: name, email address, phone number (including WhatsApp), preferred tour, dates, and group size — submitted voluntarily when you request a quote or booking.
  • Analytics: anonymized usage data such as pages visited, device type, and approximate location, collected via Google Analytics 4 or a comparable privacy-friendly analytics service, using cookies.
  • Communications: messages you send us by email, WhatsApp, or phone, which we keep on file to manage your booking and provide support.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above only to: respond to quote requests and confirm bookings; coordinate drivers, guides, and accommodation for your tour; send booking confirmations and pre-departure information; and understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

4. Cookies & Analytics

This site uses cookies to remember your language preference and to power analytics that show us which pages and tours are most useful to visitors. You can disable cookies at any time through your browser settings; the site will still function, though some preferences (like your chosen language) may not be remembered between visits.

5. Third-Party Services

We use a small number of trusted third-party services to run this site and communicate with you, including Google Analytics (usage statistics), WhatsApp (booking communication), and email/hosting providers. Each of these services processes data under its own privacy policy; we only share the minimum information needed for them to perform their function.

6. Data Retention & Security

We keep booking and correspondence records for as long as needed to manage your trip and meet our accounting and legal obligations, after which they are deleted or anonymized. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse — including serving this site exclusively over HTTPS.

7. Your Rights

You can ask us at any time to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, subject to any legal or accounting requirement to keep booking records. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to info@saharitours.com or via WhatsApp at +212 600 000 000.
