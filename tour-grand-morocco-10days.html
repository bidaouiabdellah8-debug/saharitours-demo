About This Tour

Experience the very best of Morocco on this carefully crafted 10-day journey. From the cosmopolitan city of Casablanca to the blue streets of Chefchaouen, the ancient medinas of Fes, the golden dunes of the Sahara Desert, the breathtaking Atlas Mountains, and the vibrant city of Marrakech — this tour combines Morocco's most iconic destinations into one unforgettable adventure. Whether you are fascinated by history, culture, architecture, nature, or authentic local experiences, this tour offers the perfect introduction to Morocco.

This itinerary is fully customizable — we run shorter (7-day) and longer (13 to 17-day) versions of the same grand circuit, with pickup available from Fes, Marrakech, or Tangier as well as Casablanca. Contact us to tailor the length and starting city to your trip.

10-Day Itinerary

Day 1
Casablanca Airport → Casablanca
Arrival
Welcome
Welcome to Morocco!

Meet your driver at Casablanca Mohammed V Airport and transfer to your hotel. Depending on your arrival time, visit the magnificent Hassan II Mosque — the largest mosque in Africa and one of the world's most beautiful, built on a promontory overlooking the Atlantic Ocean. Stroll along the Corniche and enjoy dinner with views of the sea.

Hassan II Mosque — the world's 3rd largest mosque, with its 210m minaret visible from sea
Corniche & Atlantic seafront — Casablanca's elegant boulevard along the ocean
Day 2
Casablanca → Rabat → Chefchaouen
Morning
Rabat
Rabat — Morocco's Royal Capital
Hassan Tower · Mausoleum of Mohammed V · Kasbah of the Udayas

After breakfast, drive to Rabat — Morocco's elegant royal and administrative capital. Visit the Hassan Tower (an unfinished 12th-century minaret), the magnificent Mausoleum of Mohammed V, and the Kasbah of the Udayas, a fortified medieval quarter overlooking the mouth of the Bou Regreg River.

Evening
Arrive
Chefchaouen — The Blue Pearl

Continue through the beautiful Rif Mountains to Chefchaouen. Spend the evening exploring the magical blue-painted streets, sipping mint tea in Uta el-Hammam Square, and losing yourself in one of the world's most photogenic medinas. Overnight in Chefchaouen.

Day 3
Chefchaouen → Volubilis → Meknes → Fes
Morning
Explore
Volubilis Roman Ruins — UNESCO Heritage

Enjoy a relaxed morning in Chefchaouen before departing. Visit the impressive Roman ruins of Volubilis — one of Morocco's finest archaeological sites, with well-preserved mosaics, triumphal arch, and temples dating from the 1st–3rd century AD. A UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Afternoon
Meknes
Meknes — The Ismaili Capital
Bab Mansour · El Hedim Square · Royal Granaries

Continue to Meknes to visit the monumental Bab Mansour gate, El Hedim Square, and the cavernous Royal Granaries of Moulay Ismail — the sultan who built a palace larger than Versailles. Drive to Fes. Overnight in Fes.

Day 4
Guided Tour of Fes
Full Day
Fes
Morocco's Oldest Imperial City — UNESCO Medina

Spend the full day exploring Fes el-Bali — the world's largest living medieval city, founded in the 9th century. Your professional guide will lead you through 9,000+ streets and alleyways unchanged for a millennium.

Royal Palace (Dar el-Makhzen) — ornate golden gates, a symbol of Moroccan craftsmanship
Jewish Quarter (Mellah) — ancient synagogues and distinctive balconied architecture
Al Quaraouiyine University — founded in 859 AD, the world's oldest continuously operating university
Chouara Tanneries — the ancient leather dyeing pits, unchanged since the medieval era
Bab Boujloud · Traditional Souks · Pottery Workshops — the living heartbeat of the ancient medina
Day 5
Fes → Ifrane → Cedar Forest → Midelt → Ziz Valley → Merzouga
07:00
Depart
Into the Middle Atlas — Ifrane, Cedar Forest & Ziz Valley

Travel through the Middle Atlas Mountains, visiting Ifrane — the "Switzerland of Morocco" with its Alpine architecture — and the Cedar Forest near Azrou, where you may meet the wild Barbary macaques. Stop for lunch in Midelt and continue through the breathtaking Ziz Valley, one of Morocco's largest palm oases. Arrive in Merzouga during the afternoon.

17:00
Arrive
Merzouga — Camel Trek at Sunset · Desert Camp

Enjoy a camel trek across the Sahara dunes before reaching your luxury desert camp. Dinner under the stars, live Berber music, and a sky blazing with the Milky Way. Overnight in the Sahara Desert camp.

Day 6
Merzouga → Rissani → Todra Gorge → Dades Valley
06:00
Sunrise
Sahara Sunrise · Rissani · Todra Gorge · Dades Valley

Wake up early to watch the sunrise over the Sahara — one of Morocco's most magical moments. Visit the historic town of Rissani. If your tour takes place on Sunday, Tuesday or Thursday, explore the famous traditional market. Continue to Todra Gorge — walk beneath 300-metre sheer limestone cliffs rising above a crystal-clear river. Drive through the scenic Dades Valley, known for its dramatic rock formations and palm groves. Dinner and overnight in a hotel overlooking the valley.

Rissani market — authentic desert souk (Sundays, Tuesdays & Thursdays)
Todra Gorge — 300m limestone walls, a world-class natural wonder
Day 7
Dades Valley → Ouarzazate → Ait Ben Haddou → Marrakech
Morning
Drive
Road of a Thousand Kasbahs → Ait Ben Haddou → Marrakech

Drive along the famous Road of a Thousand Kasbahs. Visit Ouarzazate, the "Hollywood of Africa." Explore the magnificent Kasbah of Ait Ben Haddou — a UNESCO World Heritage Site that has appeared in Gladiator, Game of Thrones, and Lawrence of Arabia. Cross the spectacular High Atlas Mountains via the Tizi n'Tichka Pass at 2,260m. Arrive in Marrakech.

Ait Ben Haddou — UNESCO ksar, one of Morocco's most iconic landmarks
Tizi n'Tichka Pass — Morocco's highest paved road at 2,260m altitude
Day 8
Guided Tour of Marrakech
Full Day
Marrakech
The Red City — Palaces, Gardens & Souks

Spend the full day exploring Marrakech with your professional guide — from the dizzying energy of Jemaa el-Fnaa to the cool shade of Majorelle Garden.

Koutoubia Mosque — the 12th-century masterpiece that inspired the Giralda in Seville
Bahia Palace — a 19th-century palace of extraordinary Moorish craftsmanship
Saadian Tombs — royal necropolis sealed for 300 years, rediscovered in 1917
Majorelle Garden — Yves Saint Laurent's iconic cobalt-blue garden paradise
Jemaa el-Fnaa Square & Traditional Souks — the beating heart of Marrakech, a UNESCO Intangible Heritage
Day 9
Marrakech at Leisure
Free Day
Relax
Your Day, Your Way

Enjoy a completely free day in Marrakech to explore at your own pace or try one of the optional activities below.

Hot Air Balloon Flight — sunrise over the Haouz Plain and Atlas Mountains (optional)
Hammam & Spa — traditional Moroccan steam bath and body scrub (optional)
Cooking Class — learn to prepare traditional Moroccan dishes (optional)
Day Trip to Ourika Valley — Atlas foothills, Berber villages, and waterfalls (optional)
Day 10
Marrakech Airport Transfer
Departure
End
Farewell Morocco

After breakfast, transfer to Marrakech Menara Airport according to your flight schedule. End of our services. We hope to welcome you back to Morocco very soon.
