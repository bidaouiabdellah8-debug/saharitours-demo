About This Tour

This is the relaxed, deluxe version of our popular Marrakech to Fes desert crossing — the same unforgettable landscapes, but spread across five unhurried days and four nights instead of four. Cross the High Atlas Mountains via the legendary Tizi n'Tichka Pass, explore UNESCO-listed Ait Ben Haddou, then wind through two extra named stops the shorter versions skip entirely — the palm-filled Skoura Oasis and the fragrant Rose Valley of Kalaa M'Gouna — before the Dades Valley, Todra Gorge, a full day dedicated to the Sahara around Merzouga, and the long, scenic drive north to imperial Fes. More time to breathe, more places to stop, and an extra overnight for travelers who don't want to rush.

Short on time or budget? Our 3-Day Express covers the same crossing in a faster, more affordable version. Prefer the classic pace? See our Classic 4-Day Marrakech to Fes via Sahara tour, or take this exact route the other way with our 5-Day Fes to Marrakech via Sahara tour.

5-Day Itinerary

Day 1
Marrakech → Ouarzazate
Marrakech → Tizi n'Tichka → Ait Ben Haddou → Ouarzazate
07:00
Depart
Early Morning Pick-up from Marrakech

Your relaxed five-day journey begins with an unhurried morning pick-up from your hotel or riad in Marrakech. Head south-east out of the city and begin the steady climb into the High Atlas Mountains, Morocco's great mountain spine separating the fertile north from the pre-Saharan south.

09:30
Cross
Tizi n'Tichka Pass — High Atlas Mountains
Altitude 2,260 m · Morocco's highest paved road pass · Berber mountain villages

Cross the spectacular High Atlas via the legendary Tizi n'Tichka Pass, the highest paved road in the country. With a relaxed schedule and no need to rush, stop along the winding switchbacks to admire sweeping mountain views, ancient Berber villages clinging to the slopes, and roadside stalls selling local minerals and geodes.

11:30
Arrive
Ait Ben Haddou — UNESCO World Heritage Site
UNESCO World Heritage · Gladiator & Game of Thrones filming location · Generous free time & lunch

Arrive at the magnificent Kasbah of Ait Ben Haddou — a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of Morocco's most iconic landmarks. This fortified ksar of earthen clay architecture rising above the Ounila River has appeared in dozens of international films and TV series, including Gladiator and Game of Thrones. With a relaxed schedule, enjoy generous free time to explore the ancient village, browse artisan workshops, and have lunch.

UNESCO heritage ksar — a living example of southern Moroccan earthen architecture, inhabited for thousands of years
Panoramic viewpoint — climb to the summit granary for a breathtaking view over the ksar and valley
14:30
Arrive
Ouarzazate — Atlas Film Studios & Taourirt Kasbah (Night 1)
"Hollywood of Africa" · World's largest film studio · Night 1 in hotel

Continue to Ouarzazate, often called the "Hollywood of Africa" — a major film production hub where Gladiator, Game of Thrones, and Lawrence of Arabia were filmed. Visit the world-famous Atlas Film Studios and/or the historic Taourirt Kasbah, a former Glaoui stronghold with ornate painted interiors. Dinner and overnight stay in Ouarzazate — the first of four nights before Fes.

Atlas Film Studios — sets and props from Gladiator, The Mummy, and Game of Thrones
Taourirt Kasbah — a former Glaoui stronghold with ornate painted interiors
Day 2
Ouarzazate → Dades Valley
Ouarzazate → Skoura Oasis → Rose Valley → Dades Valley
08:30
Depart
Road of a Thousand Kasbahs — Toward Skoura
Route des Kasbahs · Fortified mud-brick palaces · Ancient towers

After a leisurely breakfast, set out along the famous Road of a Thousand Kasbahs, lined with fortified mud-brick palaces and ancient towers — one of the highlights unique to this more relaxed itinerary, with time to actually stop and look.

10:00
Visit
Skoura Oasis
Palm-filled oasis · Numerous traditional kasbahs

Continue into the palm-filled Skoura Oasis, home to numerous traditional earthen kasbahs beneath thousands of date palms — the first of two exclusive stops that give this relaxed version its distinct, unhurried character.

12:00
Visit
Rose Valley — Kalaa M'Gouna
Kalaa M'Gouna · Rose plantations · Rosewater production

Stop in the Rose Valley around Kalaa M'Gouna — especially beautiful in flowering season — famous for its rose plantations and traditional rosewater production. A stop the shorter 3 and 4-day versions of this route simply don't have time for.

15:30
Arrive
Dades Valley — Overnight (Night 2)
Dramatic red-rock landscapes · Palm groves · Berber villages · Night 2

Continue into the Dades Valley — known for its dramatic red-rock landscapes and traditional Berber villages perched on the hillsides. Dinner and overnight stay in a charming hotel or kasbah overlooking the valley.

Day 3
Dades → Merzouga
Dades Valley → Todra Gorge → Tinghir → Rissani → Merzouga
08:00
Depart
Dades Valley — Morning Departure

After breakfast, depart through the dramatic Dades Valley, where the road winds along the Dades River through a landscape of pink and ochre rock. Enjoy the changing colours of the gorge walls as the morning light moves across the cliffs.

10:00
Arrive
Todra Gorge — 300m Limestone Cliffs
Tinghir · 300m sheer cliffs · Crystal-clear Todra River

Arrive at Todra Gorge, where towering limestone cliffs rise more than 300 metres above a crystal-clear river cutting through the rock — one of Morocco's most dramatic natural wonders. With a full itinerary and no need to rush, enjoy generous free time to walk the canyon floor and photograph the spectacular scenery.

The gorge floor walk — the narrowest point is just a few metres wide, with sheer 300m walls rising on both sides
Todra River — a ribbon of icy blue water flowing at the base of the canyon, lined with palm trees and poplar groves
12:30
Continue
Tinghir & Rissani — Historic Caravan Town
Birthplace of the Alaouite Dynasty · Authentic souk (Sun, Tue & Thu)

Continue south through Tinghir toward Rissani, the historic caravan town and birthplace of the Alaouite Dynasty. If your tour takes place on Sunday, Tuesday, or Thursday, visit the famous Rissani Traditional Market — one of the oldest and most authentic markets in Morocco, filled with spices, dates, and handcrafted goods.

16:30
Arrive
Merzouga — Camel Ride & Desert Camp (Night 3)
Erg Chebbi dunes · Up to 150m high · Night 3 in desert camp

Arrive in Merzouga during the late afternoon. Meet your camel caravan and ride across the magnificent Erg Chebbi dunes while enjoying an unforgettable desert sunset. Spend the night in a traditional or luxury desert camp with dinner, live Berber music, and stargazing under one of the world's darkest skies.

Camel trekking into Erg Chebbi at golden sunset — included in price
Desert camp — traditional Berber tent, Moroccan dinner, live music around the campfire
Day 4
Merzouga Desert Experience
A Full Day Exploring the Merzouga Desert
06:00
Sunrise
Sahara Sunrise over Erg Chebbi

Wake up early to admire one of the most beautiful sunrises in Morocco — the dunes shifting from deep purple to amber gold as the first light floods the horizon. Return from camp and enjoy a leisurely breakfast, with a full day ahead dedicated entirely to the desert.

09:30
Explore
Khamlia Village, Nomadic Families & Fossil Areas
Khamlia · Nomadic families · Fossil areas · Gnawa music

With no need to pack up and drive on, spend the morning visiting Khamlia Village, famous for its traditional Gnawa music. Meet local nomadic families, learn about their desert lifestyle and traditions, and explore the fossil-rich areas surrounding Merzouga at a relaxed, unhurried pace.

Khamlia Gnawa music — ancient ancestral rhythms unique to the deep south of Morocco
Optional quad biking — dune adventures at extra cost
15:00
Free Time
Free Afternoon Near the Dunes (Night 4)
Hotel near the dunes · Free time to relax · Night 4 in Merzouga area

In the afternoon, relax at your hotel near the dunes with genuine free time — read, swim, or simply watch the light change over Erg Chebbi, or choose optional activities such as quad biking across the dunes. Second night in the Merzouga area, in a comfortable hotel rather than another rushed transfer.

Day 5
Merzouga → Fes
Merzouga → Ziz Valley → Midelt → Ifrane → Fes
08:00
Depart
Ziz Valley — Palm Oasis & Gorge
Pre-Saharan palm oasis · Traditional ksour villages · Dramatic gorge

After breakfast, begin the long journey north through the spectacular Ziz Valley, one of Morocco's largest palm oases, with thousands of date palms stretching across the pre-Saharan landscape and traditional ksour villages carved from red earth.

11:00
Lunch
Lunch in Midelt

Stop for a relaxed lunch in Midelt — the "Apple Capital of Morocco" — a Middle Atlas mountain town at the foot of the Ayachi massif, before continuing north toward the cedar forests.

13:00
Stop
Cedar Forest — Barbary Macaques
Near Azrou · Ancient cedar ecosystem · Endemic wildlife

Continue to the Cedar Forest near Azrou, where you may encounter the famous Barbary macaques — Morocco's beloved wild primates — roaming freely through ancient trees some over 800 years old.

15:00
Stop
Ifrane — "Switzerland of Morocco"
Alpine-style architecture · 1,664m altitude · Peaceful mountain town

Arrive in Ifrane, nicknamed the "Switzerland of Morocco" for its European-style architecture, sloped rooftops, and peaceful atmosphere at 1,664m altitude. With time on your side, enjoy an unhurried stop to stretch your legs and take photos in this unusual corner of Morocco.

17:00
Arrive
Arrival in Fes — End of Tour
Drop-off at your hotel or riad in Fes

Arrive in Fes in the late afternoon or early evening, where your driver will drop you off at your hotel or riad. Your relaxed five-day Sahara adventure comes to an end — an extra day, an extra night, and two extra stops that let you actually experience Morocco instead of racing through it.
