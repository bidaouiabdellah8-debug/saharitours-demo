About This Tour

Just a short drive from Fes lie two of the Middle Atlas region's most distinctive small towns. Bhalil is a Berber village built into the hillside itself — many of its old houses are genuine troglodyte (cave) dwellings, hollowed from the rock centuries ago and still lived in today. A few kilometres further on, the walled town of Sefrou unfolds around a fast-flowing river, its old mellah (Jewish quarter) and shaded souks earning it the nickname "Morocco's cherry capital" for the orchards that surround it. This easy, unhurried day combines both towns with a scenic countryside drive, without the crowds of Morocco's bigger tourist stops.

Full-Day Itinerary

09:00
Depart
Departure from Fes
Fes → Bhalil (≈30 km · ~35 minutes)

Your driver collects you from your hotel in Fes at 09:00 and we head south through rolling countryside dotted with olive groves and small farms towards the village of Bhalil, tucked against a hillside on the edge of the Middle Atlas foothills.

09:35
Visit
Bhalil — The Cave-House Village
Old quarter walk · ~1 hour · Some cave houses still inhabited today

Bhalil's old quarter is unlike anywhere else in the region: many of its homes are troglodyte houses, carved directly into the soft rock of the hillside centuries ago and still occupied by local families. Narrow lanes and stone stairways link brightly painted facades to hidden cave interiors. With the guide's introduction, a family may welcome you inside one of these cave homes for a look at daily life (a small tip to the household is appreciated).

Genuine troglodyte houses — rock-hewn dwellings built into the hillside, some still lived in by local families
Colourful stepped streets — painted stone houses linked by small footbridges and steep alleys
Traditional djellaba-weaving — Bhalil is known locally for its hand-sewn garments and small artisan workshops
10:45
Drive
Onward to Sefrou
Bhalil → Sefrou (≈6 km · ~10 minutes)

A short hop through cherry and olive orchards brings you to Sefrou, a walled town set where the Aggaï river runs down from the Middle Atlas — long known as one of Morocco's most fertile agricultural valleys.

11:00
Visit
Sefrou Medina & the Historic Mellah
Old ramparts & gates · Former Jewish quarter · Riverside lanes

Enter Sefrou's small, easy-to-walk medina through one of its old stone gates. Wander past sections of the original ramparts to the mellah — for generations one of Morocco's most significant Jewish quarters, its tall shuttered houses and narrow lanes still standing along the river. This quieter, lived-in medina gives a very different feel from the bustling streets of Fes.

Historic mellah — the old Jewish quarter, with its distinctive tall houses and wooden balconies overlooking the river
Old city ramparts & gates — sections of Sefrou's original fortified walls, still enclosing the medina
Weekly souk — if your visit lands on souk day, farmers from the surrounding valley set up a lively market of produce, spices and household goods
12:15
Visit
Sefrou Waterfalls
Edge of the old town · Short easy walk · Great photo stop

A short walk from the medina brings you to Sefrou's small waterfalls, where the river drops through the rock right at the edge of the old town — a favourite cooling-off spot for local families and a picturesque stop for photos before lunch.

13:00
Lunch
Lunch Break in Sefrou
Local restaurant (lunch not included in price)

A relaxed lunch stop at a local restaurant in Sefrou. In cherry season (May–June), look out for the town's famous cherries served fresh or in local pastries. Budget approximately $8–12 per person.

14:00
Free
Free Time & Local Shopping
1 hour free time · Souk shopping · Photos

An hour to explore at your own pace — browse the small souk for local pottery, cherry preserves, and produce from the valley, or simply enjoy a mint tea in a quiet corner of the medina before we start back.

15:00
Return
Return to Fes
Sefrou → Fes (≈28 km · ~40 minutes) · Arrive ~18:00

We drive back to Fes through the same green countryside, arriving at your hotel around 18:00 — a relaxed, half-day-plus outing that leaves you with the evening free to enjoy Fes itself. Drop-off at your hotel or an agreed point in the city — end of tour.
