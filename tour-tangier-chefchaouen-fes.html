About This Tour

Travel from Morocco's cosmopolitan northern coast to the ancient imperial city of Fes on a memorable two-day private journey, with a night in the world-famous Blue City of Chefchaouen along the way. Ideal for travelers flying into Tangier who want to head south and inland, or for anyone who has already explored the northern coast and is ready for the mountains and medinas beyond, this private tour combines a relaxed coastal start, a magical overnight in Chefchaouen's blue alleys, and a scenic drive through the Rif foothills into Morocco's spiritual capital.

🌊 Optional Cap Spartel Stop 💙 Blue City Chefchaouen 🏔️ Rif Mountain Scenery 🕌 Imperial City of Fes 🚗 Private & Flexible

2-Day Itinerary

1
Day One
Departure 08:30 am
Tangier → Chefchaouen — Northern Coast to the Blue City
Tangier → Chefchaouen (≈2 hours) · Overnight in Chefchaouen (accommodation not included)

Private driver pick-up from your hotel in Tangier in the morning. Since you're already in the city, we can begin with a quick optional stop at Cap Spartel or one of Tangier's viewpoints before heading out — a natural first stop before the inland drive south toward Chefchaouen, roughly 2 hours away. On arrival in Chefchaouen, enjoy free time to explore the Blue Medina, its indigo-washed alleys, Plaza Uta el-Hammam and the local handicraft shops, with an optional traditional lunch along the way. In the afternoon, relax and take in the atmosphere, with the option of a walk up to the Spanish Mosque viewpoint for a sunset view over the entire Blue City. Overnight in Chefchaouen (accommodation not included — travelers book their own).

Cap Spartel (optional) — a quick stop where the Atlantic meets the Mediterranean before leaving Tangier
Blue Medina — the maze of indigo-washed alleys that made Chefchaouen world-famous
Plaza Uta el-Hammam — the lively main square with the Kasbah museum and shaded gardens
Local handicraft shops — woven blankets, leather goods and artisan workshops in the blue alleys
Spanish Mosque (optional) — panoramic sunset viewpoint over the entire Blue City
2
Day Two
After breakfast
Chefchaouen → Fes — Return to the Imperial City
Chefchaouen → Fes via the Rif foothills (≈200 km · ~3.5 hours)

After breakfast, depart Chefchaouen and drive south through the scenic Rif foothills toward Fes. Arrival in Fes in the afternoon, where your private driver drops you at your hotel or riad — or at the airport, if that's your onward plan. Travelers with extra time on arrival can add on a guided Fes medina tour to discover Bab Boujloud, the Chouara Tannery and the Al Quaraouiyine quarter.

Rif Mountain foothills — a scenic drive south through green valleys and Amazigh villages
Arrival in Fes — drop-off at your hotel, riad, or Fes-Saïss Airport
Optional add-on — a guided tour of the Fes medina for travelers with extra time — end of tour
