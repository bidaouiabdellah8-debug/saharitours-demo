About This Tour

Fes is just a 30-minute drive from Meknes, making it the easiest possible day trip — and one of the richest. Its medina, Fes el-Bali, is the world's largest car-free urban area and a UNESCO World Heritage site, packed with over a thousand years of history. This full day gives you extra time to properly explore the tanneries, the world's oldest university, historic fondouks, bustling souks, and a sweeping sunset-style viewpoint over the entire city from the Merinid Tombs — all without an overnight stay.

Full-Day Itinerary

09:00
Depart
Departure from Meknes
Meknes → Fes (≈60 km · ~30 minutes)

A relaxed morning pick-up from your hotel in Meknes — with Fes so close, there's no need for an early start. A short, easy drive brings you straight to the gates of the medina.

09:30
Enter
Bab Boujloud, the Blue Gate
Gateway to the world's largest car-free urban area

Enter Fes el-Bali through the ornate blue-and-green tiled Bab Boujloud, the medina's grand entrance, and step directly into a maze of over 9,000 alleyways that make up the world's largest car-free urban area — a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1981.

10:00
Visit
Chouara Tannery Viewpoint
The oldest leather tannery in the world · Colourful dye pits

Look out over the famous Chouara Tannery from one of the surrounding leather-shop terraces — hundreds of stone vats filled with dyes in vivid reds, yellows, and blues, worked using methods essentially unchanged since medieval times. A true feast for the eyes (and the nose — mint sprigs are provided!).

10:45
Visit
Al Quaraouiyine & the Nejjarine Fondouk
World's oldest university & library · Historic caravanserai

Pass the exterior of Al Quaraouiyine, founded in 859 AD and recognised by UNESCO and Guinness World Records as the oldest continuously operating university and library on Earth. Continue to the Nejjarine Museum and Fondouk, a beautifully restored 18th-century caravanserai housing a museum of wooden arts and crafts, then wander the bustling souks of metalwork, leather, and ceramics.

Al Quaraouiyine — the world's oldest continuously operating university and library, founded in 859 AD
Nejjarine Fondouk — a restored 18th-century caravanserai with a museum of wooden arts and crafts
Bustling souks — metalwork, leather, ceramics, and spice stalls throughout the winding lanes
12:30
Lunch
Lunch in the Medina
Traditional restaurant or rooftop terrace (lunch not included)

Enjoy lunch at a traditional restaurant or rooftop terrace inside the medina, with views over the maze of rooftops that makes up Fes el-Bali.

14:00
Visit
Fes el-Jdid, Royal Palace & Merinid Tombs
Golden Royal Palace gates · Panoramic city viewpoint

Visit Fes el-Jdid and the ornate golden gates of the Royal Palace (Dar al-Makhzen), still an official royal residence today. Finish the day at the Merinid Tombs, ruined 14th-century royal tombs on a hilltop above the medina, offering the single best panoramic view over the entire city of Fes.

16:00
Return
Return to Meknes
Fes → Meknes (≈60 km · ~30 minutes) · Arrive ~18:00

A short, easy drive back to Meknes, arriving at your hotel by early evening — end of tour.
