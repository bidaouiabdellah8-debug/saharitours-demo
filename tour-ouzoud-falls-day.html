About This Tour

The Ouzoud Falls are among the tallest waterfalls in North Africa, plunging roughly 110 meters over a series of red rock cliffs into the deep Al-Abid gorge below. Surrounded by terraced olive groves that give the falls their name ("ouzoud" means "the act of grinding olives" in the local Amazigh dialect), this is one of the most refreshing and scenic day trips out of Marrakech — a world away from the desert heat, with mist, rainbows, wild Barbary macaques, and walking trails that lead right down to the water's edge.

Full-Day Itinerary

08:00
Depart
Departure from Marrakech
Marrakech → Ouzoud (≈150 km · ~2.5 hours)

Your driver picks you up from your hotel or riad in Marrakech and we head northeast through the Atlas foothills, passing small villages and increasingly green, terraced countryside as we approach the Azilal region.

10:30
Arrive
Arrival & Walk Down to the Falls
Walking trail through olive groves · Multiple viewpoints

Arrive at Ouzoud and set off on foot along the shaded trail that winds down through centuries-old olive groves, with the sound of falling water growing louder at every turn. Along the way, a series of viewpoints reveal the falls from different angles, cascading over the red rock cliffs into the gorge below.

North Africa's tallest falls — water tumbling roughly 110 meters over layered red rock in multiple cascades
Terraced olive groves — shaded walking paths through centuries-old olive trees that give the falls their name
11:30
Explore
Base of the Falls, the Al-Abid Gorge & Barbary Macaques
Al-Abid river gorge · Wild Barbary macaques · Optional boat ride

Continue down to the base of the falls, where the Al-Abid River carves through the gorge and mist drifts up from the plunge pools, often catching the sunlight in small rainbows. This area is home to a troop of wild Barbary macaques, one of the few places in Morocco where you can see these monkeys in a semi-natural setting close to visitors. For a small extra fee, a short traditional boat ride takes you out onto the pool right at the foot of the falls for an up-close view and photos.

Wild Barbary macaques — often seen resting or playing in the trees near the base of the falls
Optional boat ride — a short paddle out to the base of the cascades for the classic up-close photo (small extra cost)
Al-Abid gorge — dramatic red rock walls carved by the river, with mist and rainbows near the plunge pools
13:00
Lunch
Lunch Break — Terrace over the Falls
Restaurant with a direct view of the waterfalls (lunch not included in price)

Enjoy lunch at one of the terraced restaurants built right into the cliffside, with some of the best waterfall views in the area. A relaxed spot to enjoy a tagine while watching the falls tumble past. Budget approximately $10–15 per person.

14:30
Free
Free Time — Trails & Photography
1.5 hours free · Additional viewpoints · Local craft stalls

Free time to explore further along the marked trails, cross to the opposite side of the gorge for different photo angles, or simply relax by the water. Small stalls near the falls sell local pottery, olive oil, and honey — a nice opportunity for souvenirs directly from the producing region.

16:00
Return
Return to Marrakech
Ouzoud → Marrakech (≈150 km · ~2.5 hours) · Arrive ~19:00

Head back on the road to Marrakech through the Atlas foothills as the afternoon light softens over the countryside. Arrive in Marrakech around 19:00 pm. Drop-off at your hotel or riad — end of tour.
