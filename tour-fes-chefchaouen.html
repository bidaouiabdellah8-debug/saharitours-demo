3-Day Itinerary

1
Day One
Departure 08:30 am
Fes → Chefchaouen
Fes → Rif Mountains → Chefchaouen · Overnight in Chefchaouen

Depart from Fes heading north through the scenic Rif Mountains, with photo stops along the way to admire the lush green valleys and mountain panoramas. Arrive in Chefchaouen — the legendary "Blue Pearl" — and spend the afternoon and evening exploring its magical blue-washed streets.

Plaza Uta el-Hammam — the vibrant heart of Chefchaouen, lined with cafes and the grand mosque
Andalusian Kasbah — 15th-century historical fortress at the center of the medina
Old Medina — narrow blue alleyways, traditional markets, and artisan workshops
Ras el-Ma — a natural spring at the edge of the medina, perfect for relaxing
Spanish Mosque (optional) — 20-minute hike rewarded with a panoramic view over the whole blue city
2
Day Two
After breakfast
Chefchaouen → Rabat
Chefchaouen → Rabat (Royal Capital) · Overnight in Rabat

After breakfast, depart for Rabat — Morocco's elegant royal capital and one of the country's four imperial cities. Spend the afternoon exploring its rich historical heritage, from ancient ruins to stunning mosques, ending with a stroll along the Atlantic corniche.

Hassan Tower — the iconic unfinished minaret, one of Morocco's most recognizable landmarks
Mohammed V Mausoleum — a masterpiece of Moroccan architecture, final resting place of King Mohammed V and King Hassan II
Kasbah of the Udayas — a stunning hilltop fortress overlooking the Atlantic and the Bou Regreg River
Old Medina — traditional souks, Moroccan handicrafts, and authentic street food
Bou Regreg Corniche — a scenic riverside promenade with beautiful views of Salé across the river
3
Day Three
After breakfast
Rabat → Casablanca → Airport
Rabat → Casablanca → Mohammed V International Airport

After breakfast in Rabat, head to Casablanca — Morocco's economic capital and largest city. Discover the striking contrast between traditional Moroccan culture and modern urban energy, before your transfer to Mohammed V International Airport to conclude the tour.

Hassan II Mosque — the largest mosque in Morocco, third largest in the world, with a stunning ocean-side location on the Atlantic
Mohammed V Square — the historic and administrative heart of the city, surrounded by elegant colonial architecture
Habous Quarter — a charming traditional neighbourhood blending Moroccan style with modern architecture
Ain Diab Corniche — a beautiful coastal strip of restaurants, cafes, and Atlantic views before heading to the airport
Transfer to Mohammed V International Airport — end of tour
