About This Tour

Escape the medina and breathe the crisp mountain air of the Middle Atlas — Morocco's forested highland heartland, a world apart from the desert and the cities. This full-day tour from Fes takes you through winding mountain roads past Amazigh villages, stopping at a spectacular natural cave, the famously clean and beautiful city of Ifrane, ancient cedar forests where wild Barbary macaques roam free, and a high panoramic viewpoint above the mountain town of Azrou. A perfect day for families, nature lovers, and photography enthusiasts.

Pickup is also available from Meknes. Travelling from further afield? Ask about combining this with our Bhalil & Sefrou day trip for a longer Middle Atlas exploration.

Full-Day Itinerary

08:00
Depart
Departure from Fes
Fes → Imouzzer des Marmoucha (≈80 km · mountain road)

Depart from your hotel in Fes heading south towards the Middle Atlas. The mountain road climbs gradually through the Saïss Plain, then into rolling hills dotted with traditional Amazigh (Berber) villages, cedar groves, and spectacular landscapes — a beautiful drive before your first stop.

09:30
Arrive
Imouzzer Cave
Imouzzer region · Natural cave · Mountain setting

Visit the impressive natural cave at Imouzzer, nestled in the Middle Atlas mountains. The cave features remarkable rock formations shaped over millennia, a serene mountain atmosphere, and breathtaking surroundings — perfect for photography and a peaceful start to the day.

Natural rock formations — dramatic stalactites, mineral deposits, and geological features carved by water over thousands of years
Mountain panoramas — the surrounding landscape of green hills and Atlas peaks provides a stunning backdrop for photos
Peaceful atmosphere — a calm, uncrowded setting far from city noise — ideal for relaxed exploration and nature photography
11:00
Arrive
Ifrane — "Switzerland of Morocco"
Altitude: 1,664 m · Cleanest city in Africa · Visit duration: approx. 1h 30 min

Arrive in Ifrane — Morocco's most surprising city. Built in 1929 by the French as a mountain resort, its Alpine-style chalets, manicured parks, and spotless streets make it look like it was lifted straight from Switzerland and placed in the Atlas mountains. At 1,664m altitude, it's noticeably cooler and fresher than Fes.

The Ifrane Lion — the famous stone lion sculpture carved by a German prisoner of war in 1945, symbol of the city
Beautiful parks & gardens — the immaculate public gardens with flower beds, walking paths, and pine-covered surroundings
European-style town centre — red-roofed chalets and wide clean avenues unlike anywhere else in Morocco
Free time — stroll at your own pace, enjoy a coffee in the mountain air, or browse local shops
12:30
Drive
Cedar Forests & Barbary Macaques
Near Azrou · Morocco's largest cedar forest · Endemic Barbary macaques

Head towards Azrou and into Morocco's most famous cedar forests — some of the oldest and largest in North Africa. These ancient cedars, some over 800 years old, are home to the endangered Barbary macaque (Macaca sylvanus), found only in Morocco and Algeria. Watch the monkeys move freely through the treetops and on the forest floor in their natural habitat.

Barbary macaques in the wild — the friendliest wild primates in Morocco, Morocco's most beloved forest residents
Ancient cedar trees — some specimens over 800 years old, massive trunks towering above the forest floor
Fresh mountain air — the scent of cedar and pine at 1,500m altitude, a world away from the city
13:30
Lunch
Lunch Break
Local restaurant in the mountain area (not included in price)

Enjoy a relaxed lunch at a local restaurant in the mountain setting. Fresh mountain trout is a speciality of this region — your guide will take you to an authentic local spot away from tourist traps.

15:00
Arrive
Azrou — Mountain Town
Altitude: 1,250 m · Amazigh Berber town · Artisan crafts

Arrive in Azrou — a genuine Amazigh (Berber) mountain town, far less touristy than Ifrane and all the more authentic for it. Named after the giant black volcanic rock (the word "azrou" means "rock" in Tamazight) at its heart, the town is known for its traditional crafts and its honest, local character.

The Artisan Cooperative — Azrou is famous for its wood crafts, carpets, and pottery; one of the best places in Morocco to buy authentic Amazigh handicrafts
Traditional markets (souks) — browse a local mountain souk without tourist pressure, for carpets, cedar wood items, and Berber silver
Mountain atmosphere — the genuine daily life of an Amazigh community in the heart of the Atlas
16:00
View
Panoramic Viewpoint
Hilltop above Azrou · 360° view over the Middle Atlas

A short drive to one of the elevated viewpoints above Azrou offers a sweeping panorama over the town and the surrounding Middle Atlas mountains. With the late afternoon light bathing the cedar forests in gold, this is the perfect final photo stop of the day before heading back to Fes.

360° mountain panorama — breathtaking views over Azrou, the cedar forests, and the rolling Atlas ridgelines
Golden hour photography — the late-afternoon light over the Atlas is truly exceptional for landscape photos
17:00
Return
Return to Fes
Azrou → Fes (approx. 80 km · 1h 30 min) · Arrive 18:30–19:00

Depart Azrou and return comfortably to Fes, arriving between 18:30 and 19:00 pm depending on traffic. Drop-off at your hotel or an agreed meeting point — end of tour.
