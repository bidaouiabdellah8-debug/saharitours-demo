About This Tour

Rabat is Morocco's seat of government — an elegant, spacious capital on the Atlantic coast that mixes royal monuments, a fortified river-mouth kasbah, and quiet Roman ruins with almost none of the crowds you'll find in Fes or Marrakech. Departing directly from Meknes, this full-day round trip takes you west to the unfinished 12th-century Hassan Tower and the Mausoleum of Mohammed V, into the blue-and-white lanes of the Kasbah of the Udayas overlooking the ocean, and through the stork-filled Chellah necropolis, before returning to Meknes the same evening.

Full-Day Itinerary

07:30
Depart
Departure from Meknes
Meknes → Rabat (≈140 km · ~2 hours)

Your driver picks you up early from your hotel in Meknes for the drive west across the plains toward the Atlantic coast, arriving in Rabat by mid-morning.

09:30
Visit
Hassan Tower & the Mausoleum of Mohammed V
Unfinished 12th-century minaret · Royal mausoleum · ~1 hour

Begin at the Hassan Tower, the towering unfinished minaret commissioned in the late 12th century by Sultan Yacoub al-Mansour for what would have been the world's largest mosque — construction stopped at his death, leaving rows of stone columns standing in the open air. Beside it stands the Mausoleum of Mohammed V, a striking white marble monument guarded by the Royal Guard, where Morocco's king and his father are laid to rest.

Hassan Tower — the unfinished 12th-century minaret and its field of stone columns, one of Rabat's most iconic landmarks
Mausoleum of Mohammed V — white marble and green-tiled roof, final resting place of Kings Mohammed V and Hassan II
Royal Guard — mounted and standing guards in traditional dress at the mausoleum entrance
11:00
Visit
Kasbah of the Udayas & Andalusian Gardens
Blue-and-white lanes · Ocean viewpoint · Andalusian Gardens

Continue to the fortified Kasbah of the Udayas, perched where the Bou Regreg river meets the Atlantic. Wander its blue-and-white painted lanes — reminiscent of Chefchaouen, but with sea air instead of mountain air — before stepping into the peaceful Andalusian Gardens and out onto the ramparts for sweeping views over the ocean and the neighbouring town of Salé across the river.

Blue-and-white streets — narrow lanes of the kasbah's residential quarter, freshly painted each year
Andalusian Gardens — a tranquil walled garden of citrus trees and fountains inside the kasbah walls
Ramparts & ocean viewpoint — panoramic views over the Atlantic and the mouth of the Bou Regreg river
12:30
Lunch
Lunch Break in Rabat
Restaurant near the kasbah or riverfront (lunch not included in price)

Enjoy lunch at a local restaurant near the kasbah or along the riverfront — a good spot to try fresh Atlantic seafood. Budget approximately $12–18 per person.

13:30
Visit
Chellah Necropolis
Roman ruins & Merinid necropolis · Stork-nested minaret · Walled gardens

Step into Chellah, a walled complex built over the ancient Roman city of Sala Colonia and later turned into a royal necropolis by the Merinid dynasty. Wander among crumbling Roman columns, medieval tombs, and a ruined minaret where storks build their nests every year — one of the most atmospheric and least crowded sites in the capital.

15:00
Walk
Optional Medina Stroll
Rabat medina & Avenue Mohammed V · Free time · ~1 hour

A short walk through Rabat's medina and along the tree-lined Avenue Mohammed V, past government buildings and cafés — a glimpse of the orderly, administrative side of Morocco. Free time for a coffee or last-minute shopping before the drive home.

16:00
Return
Return to Meknes
Rabat → Meknes (≈140 km · ~2 hours) · Arrive ~18:00-19:00

Depart Rabat and drive back east to Meknes as the afternoon winds down, arriving in the early evening. Drop-off at your hotel or an agreed point in the city — end of tour.
