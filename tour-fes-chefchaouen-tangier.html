About This Tour

Discover the beauty of northern Morocco on a memorable two-day journey from the historic city of Fes to the famous Blue City of Chefchaouen and the cosmopolitan city of Tangier. This private tour combines breathtaking mountain landscapes, traditional Moroccan culture, charming medinas, panoramic viewpoints and the unique atmosphere of Morocco's northern coast.

💙 Blue City Chefchaouen 🌊 Atlantic Meets Mediterranean 🏛️ Tangier Kasbah & Medina 🕳️ Caves of Hercules 🚗 Private & Flexible

2-Day Itinerary

1
Day One
Departure 08:30 am
Fes → Chefchaouen — Explore the Blue City
Fes → Chefchaouen · Overnight in Chefchaouen (accommodation not included)

Private driver pick-up from your hotel or riad in Fes in the morning, then departure toward Chefchaouen with a scenic drive through northern Morocco, including photo and countryside stops along the way. Arrival in Chefchaouen, the Blue City nestled in the Rif Mountains and famous for its blue-painted houses, narrow streets and peaceful atmosphere. Enjoy free time to explore the Old Medina, its blue alleys, local handicrafts, traditional shops and small cafés — with an optional traditional Moroccan lunch in the medina. In the afternoon, relax and soak in the atmosphere, with the option to walk up to the Spanish Mosque viewpoint for panoramic views over the Blue City, especially beautiful at sunset. Overnight in Chefchaouen (accommodation not included — travelers book their own).

Blue Medina — the maze of indigo-washed alleys that made Chefchaouen world-famous
Plaza Uta el-Hammam — the lively main square with the Kasbah museum and shaded gardens
Ras El Maa spring — the natural mountain spring at the edge of the medina
Local handicraft shops — woven blankets, leather goods and artisan workshops in the blue alleys
Spanish Mosque (optional) — panoramic sunset viewpoint over the entire Blue City
2
Day Two
After breakfast
Chefchaouen → Tangier — Tangier Highlights
Chefchaouen → Tangier (≈112 km · ~2 hours)

After breakfast, depart Chefchaouen for Tangier — around 112 km and roughly 2 hours on the road. On arrival, begin your private sightseeing tour: the historic Kasbah, the old Medina, traditional streets and markets, and the lively Grand Socco. Continue on to Tangier's famous coastal attractions, Cap Spartel — with its spectacular views over the point where the Atlantic Ocean meets the Mediterranean Sea — and the Caves of Hercules, one of the most popular attractions around Tangier. Enjoy a traditional Moroccan lunch or fresh seafood in Tangier. In the afternoon, your private driver drops you at your final destination in Tangier — your hotel, the train station, or Tangier Ibn Battouta Airport.

Tangier Kasbah — the historic hilltop fortress overlooking the Strait of Gibraltar
Old Medina & Grand Socco — traditional streets, markets and Tangier's iconic main square
Cap Spartel — the spectacular viewpoint where the Atlantic Ocean meets the Mediterranean Sea
Caves of Hercules — one of the most popular natural attractions around Tangier
Drop-off in Tangier — your hotel, the train station, or Tangier Ibn Battouta Airport — end of tour
