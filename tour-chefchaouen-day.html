About This Tour

Chefchaouen — the world-famous "Blue Pearl of Morocco" — is one of the most photographed cities on earth. Nestled in the Rif Mountains, its medina is painted in extraordinary shades of indigo and sky blue, making every alley, doorway, and staircase a work of art. This full-day round trip from Fes takes you through the scenic Rif Mountain range, then plunges you into the blue labyrinth of Chefchaouen for hours of free exploration, lunch, photography, and a grand finale panoramic view from the Spanish Mosque.

Pickup is also available from Meknes, and directly from Tangier via our Tangier day-trip route through Martil, M'diq & Tetouan.

Full-Day Itinerary

08:00
Depart
Departure from Fes
Fes → Chefchaouen via the Rif Mountains (≈200 km · ~3 hours)

Your driver picks you up from your hotel in Fes and we head northwest towards the Rif Mountains. The road winds through increasingly dramatic mountain scenery — deep valleys, cedar slopes, Amazigh villages clinging to hillsides, and spectacular views that open up at every bend.

10:30
Photo
Rif Mountains Panoramic Stop
High-altitude viewpoint · 15–20 min stop

A short stop at one of the elevated panoramic viewpoints along the Rif Mountain road — a rare opportunity to stretch your legs, breathe the mountain air, and capture the wild beauty of the Rif landscape before descending into Chefchaouen.

Sweeping mountain panorama — layered Rif ridgelines stretching to the horizon, valleys below
Traditional Berber villages — stone and adobe houses on steep hillsides, an ancient way of life
12:00
Arrive
Arrival in Chefchaouen — The Blue City
Medina entry · Free exploration begins · #1 photographed city in Morocco

Arrive in Chefchaouen and enter the medina on foot — and the Blue City makes its first extraordinary impression. Every surface is painted in shades of blue: cornflower, cobalt, azure, indigo. Cobblestone alleys twist and climb between blue-washed walls hung with flowers, terracotta pots, and hand-woven rugs. Begin free exploration of this living work of art.

The famous blue alleys — every corner is a photo opportunity unlike anywhere else in the world
Artisan quarter — hand-woven djellabas, Berber blankets, leather goods, and local spices in the covered souks
Cascading flower pots — every balcony and doorstep is decorated with geraniums and bougainvillea against the blue walls
12:30
Visit
Uta el-Hammam Square & Kasbah
Heart of the medina · Historic Kasbah (15th century) · Traditional cafés

Reach Place Uta el-Hammam — the beating heart of Chefchaouen. This beautiful main square is ringed by café terraces, the octagonal minaret of the Grand Mosque, and the entrance to the Kasbah — the historic fortress built by Moulay Ali Ben Rachid in 1471 when he founded the city.

Historic Kasbah (1471) — the 15th-century fortress with lush gardens and a small museum of Moroccan art and heritage
Grand Mosque — the distinctive octagonal minaret, unique to Chefchaouen, visible from across the square
Terrace cafés — enjoy a mint tea or Moroccan coffee at one of the square's traditional café terraces watching local life flow by
14:00
Lunch
Lunch Break — Traditional Moroccan Meal
Restaurant with view over the old medina (lunch not included in price)

Enjoy a traditional Moroccan lunch at a restaurant with views over the medina's blue rooftops. Your driver-guide will recommend the best honest local spots — try the lamb tagine with prunes and almonds, or the goat cheese local to this mountain region. Budget approximately $10–15 per person.

15:00
Free
Free Time — Photography & Exploration
1.5 hours free in the medina · Shopping · Photos · Wandering

An hour and a half to do whatever you love most in Chefchaouen. Wander into the quieter back alleys that tourists rarely reach. Browse the wool-weaving workshops and leather goods stalls. Or simply sit with a tea and let the extraordinary beauty of this blue world wash over you.

Photography — hidden doorways, stairways, cats in the alleys, flower pots and blue walls: countless memorable shots
Local shopping — hand-woven woollen djellabas, Berber blankets, cedar wood crafts, argan products, and local honey
Ras el-Ma spring — a short walk to the refreshing mountain spring at the edge of the medina, where women still wash laundry
16:30
View
Spanish Mosque Viewpoint
Hilltop above Chefchaouen · 20 min uphill walk · Best panoramic view in the city

The grand finale of the day: climb the hill above Chefchaouen to the abandoned Spanish Mosque — the most famous viewpoint in the city. From here, the entire Blue City spreads below you in a breathtaking panorama: blue rooftops tumbling down the mountain, the minaret of the Grand Mosque, the surrounding Rif ridgelines, and (on clear days) distant glimpses of the Mediterranean coast.

Iconic panoramic view — the most photographed viewpoint in all of Chefchaouen, with the entire blue medina laid out below
The Spanish Mosque — abandoned since it was built by the Spanish in the 1920s, now a photogenic ruin with history
Rif Mountain ridgelines — the surrounding peaks frame the city, with deep valleys and forests visible in every direction
17:30
Return
Return to Fes
Chefchaouen → Fes via the Rif Mountains (≈200 km · ~3h 30 min) · Arrive ~21:00

Depart Chefchaouen and begin the drive back through the Rif Mountains to Fes — a beautiful journey as the evening light fades over the mountains. Arrive in Fes around 21:00 pm. Drop-off at your hotel or an agreed point in the city — end of tour.
