About This Tour

For travelers who want to see a Morocco beyond the postcards, Bhalil and Sefrou are two of the most authentic, least-visited towns near Meknes. Bhalil is a centuries-old troglodyte village where cave houses built into the hillside are still lived in today, while nearby Sefrou — Morocco's "cherry capital" — hides a historic Jewish mellah, ancient ramparts, and seasonal waterfalls behind its unassuming streets. This full-day trip, departing directly from Meknes via Fes, is a rewarding glimpse of everyday, non-touristy Morocco.

Full-Day Itinerary

09:00
Depart
Departure from Meknes
Meknes → Bhalil via Fes (≈75 km · ~1.25 hours)

Morning pick-up from your hotel in Meknes, driving east past Fes and on toward the small hillside village of Bhalil.

10:15
Visit
Bhalil, the Troglodyte Village
Inhabited cave houses · ~1 hour

Walk through Bhalil, a centuries-old village where many homes are built directly into the hillside as troglodyte cave dwellings, still lived in by local families today. Where welcomed, glimpse inside a traditional cave home to see how these unique dwellings stay naturally cool in summer and warm in winter.

11:30
Drive
Continue to Sefrou
Bhalil → Sefrou (≈8 km · ~15 minutes)

A short scenic drive to nearby Sefrou, known across Morocco as the country's "cherry capital" and once a key stop on the historic caravan trade route.

11:45
Visit
Sefrou's Historic Mellah & Ramparts
Historic Jewish quarter · Ancient city walls

Explore Sefrou's mellah, the historic Jewish quarter that was once one of the largest in Morocco, its old synagogues and courtyard houses still standing quietly among the narrow lanes. Walk a stretch of the town's ancient ramparts, offering a quiet, uncrowded contrast to the busier medinas of Fes and Meknes.

The Mellah — Sefrou's historic Jewish quarter, once home to a thriving community, with quiet courtyard houses and old synagogues
City ramparts — a stretch of Sefrou's ancient defensive walls, quiet and largely undiscovered by tourists
12:30
Visit
Sefrou Waterfalls (Seasonal)
Just outside town · Flow depends on season

A short stop at Sefrou's small waterfalls just outside town, a pleasant, shaded local beauty spot — best after the winter and spring rains, and often reduced to a trickle in the dry summer months.

13:00
Lunch
Lunch in Sefrou
Local restaurant (lunch not included in price)

Enjoy lunch at a local restaurant in Sefrou, a great chance to try home-style Moroccan cooking away from the tourist-priced restaurants of the bigger cities.

14:30
Return
Return to Meknes
Sefrou → Meknes via Fes · Arrive ~16:00-17:00

Drive back west through Fes to Meknes, arriving at your hotel in the mid-to-late afternoon — end of tour.
