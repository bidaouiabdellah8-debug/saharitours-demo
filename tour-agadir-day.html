About This Tour

Agadir is Morocco's premier Atlantic beach resort — a city almost entirely rebuilt in clean, modern lines after the devastating 1960 earthquake, now known for its long sandy promenade, palm-lined avenues, and relaxed seaside pace. This full-day round trip from Marrakech crosses the western High Atlas to reach the coast, with a panoramic stop at the hilltop Kasbah Oufella ruins overlooking the bay before you have free time to explore the marina, the souk, and the beach itself.

🌊 Atlantic Beach City 🏰 Kasbah Oufella Ruins ⛵ Marina & Promenade 🛍️ Souk El Had 📸 Panoramic Bay Views

Full-Day Itinerary

07:30
Depart
Early Departure from Marrakech
Marrakech → Agadir via the Western High Atlas (≈250 km · ~2h 45min–3h)

An early start is needed for this longer coastal trip. Your driver picks you up from your hotel and heads southwest, climbing over a pass of the western High Atlas before descending toward the Atlantic — a route through changing scenery, from mountain villages to argan tree groves as you near the coast.

10:30
Arrive
Kasbah Oufella — Hilltop Panorama
Hilltop above the bay · 45 min stop · Best photo spot in Agadir

Our first stop is the ruined Kasbah Oufella, perched on the hill above the city. Originally built in the 16th century and badly damaged by the 1960 earthquake, its walls and gate now serve mainly as a viewpoint — but what a viewpoint: the entire sweep of Agadir's beach, harbor, and modern skyline laid out below, with the Atlantic stretching to the horizon.

Panoramic bay view — the full curve of Agadir's beach and harbor from the ramparts
Historic ruins — restored kasbah walls and gate dating to the 16th century
11:30
Visit
Agadir Marina
Yachts, cafés & boutiques on the waterfront

Down from the kasbah, wander the modern marina district — a pleasant, palm-lined waterfront lined with yachts, seafood restaurants, and small boutiques, very different in feel from the older medinas of Fes or Marrakech.

12:30
Lunch
Lunch Break — Fresh Seafood
Near the marina or Souk El Had (lunch not included in price)

Agadir's Atlantic location means excellent, freshly-caught seafood. Your driver-guide can suggest honest local spots near the port for grilled fish and seafood tagines. Budget approximately $10–15 per person.

13:30
Visit
Souk El Had — The Traditional Market
One of Morocco's largest covered souks · Free browsing

Rebuilt after the earthquake, Souk El Had is now one of the largest covered markets in Morocco, with thousands of stalls selling spices, leather, argan products, textiles, and souvenirs — a lively contrast to Agadir's otherwise modern character.

15:00
Free
Free Time on the Beach Promenade
~1.5 hours free · Long sandy beach & palm promenade

Free time to walk (or relax) along Agadir's long, wide sandy beach and its palm-fringed promenade — one of the best-maintained beachfronts in Morocco. A great chance to feel the sea breeze before the drive back over the mountains.

16:30
Return
Return to Marrakech
Agadir → Marrakech via the High Atlas (≈250 km · ~3 hours) · Arrive ~20:00

Depart Agadir and head back over the High Atlas as the sun begins to set over the mountains. Arrive in Marrakech around 20:00 pm. Drop-off at your hotel or an agreed point in the city — end of tour.
