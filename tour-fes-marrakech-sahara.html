About This Tour

Embark on an extraordinary 4-day journey from the imperial city of Fes to the vibrant city of Marrakech, crossing the breathtaking Middle Atlas Mountains, the golden dunes of the Sahara Desert, ancient kasbahs, dramatic canyons, and picturesque valleys. This carefully designed tour combines adventure, culture, history, and spectacular scenery — one of Morocco's most popular and rewarding travel experiences.

Prefer to start and end in the same city? Our Marrakech Desert Loop runs the same Sahara highlights as a 3 or 4-day round trip from Marrakech — no one-way drop-off in Fes required.

4-Day Itinerary

Day 1
Fes → Merzouga
Fes → Ifrane → Cedar Forest → Midelt → Ziz Valley → Merzouga
07:00
Depart
Early Morning Pick-up from Fes

Your adventure begins with an early morning pick-up from your hotel or riad in Fes. Travel through the scenic Middle Atlas Mountains, stopping in Ifrane, famous for its alpine architecture and peaceful atmosphere at 1,664m altitude.

10:00
Stop
Cedar Forest — Barbary Macaques
Near Azrou · Ancient cedar ecosystem · Endemic wildlife

Continue to the Cedar Forest near Azrou, where you may encounter the famous Barbary macaques — Morocco's beloved wild primates — roaming freely through ancient trees some over 800 years old.

13:00
Lunch
Lunch in Midelt

After lunch in Midelt — the "Apple Capital of Morocco" — drive through the spectacular Ziz Valley, one of Morocco's largest palm oases, with thousands of date palms stretching across the pre-Saharan landscape and traditional ksour villages carved from red earth.

17:00
Arrive
Merzouga — Camel Ride & Desert Camp
Erg Chebbi dunes · Up to 150m high · Night 1 in desert camp

Arrive in Merzouga during the afternoon. Meet your camel caravan and ride across the magnificent Erg Chebbi dunes while enjoying an unforgettable desert sunset. Spend the night in a traditional or luxury desert camp with dinner, live Berber music, and stargazing under one of the world's darkest skies.

Camel trekking into Erg Chebbi at golden sunset — included in price
Desert camp — traditional Berber tent, Moroccan dinner, live music around the campfire
Day 2
Explore the Sahara
Merzouga · Sunrise · Khamlia · Desert Culture
06:00
Sunrise
Sahara Sunrise over Erg Chebbi

Wake up early to admire one of the most beautiful sunrises in Morocco — the dunes shifting from deep purple to amber gold as the first light floods the horizon. After breakfast, discover the wonders of the Merzouga region.

09:00
Explore
Khamlia Village, Nomadic Families & Desert Activities
Khamlia · Nomadic families · Fossil areas · Night 2 in hotel

Visit Khamlia Village, famous for its traditional Gnawa music. Meet local nomadic families and learn about their desert lifestyle and traditions. Explore the fossil areas around Merzouga and enjoy panoramic views of the Sahara. In the afternoon, relax at your hotel near the dunes or choose optional activities such as ATV or buggy rides across the dunes.

Khamlia Gnawa music — ancient ancestral rhythms unique to the deep south of Morocco
Optional ATV / buggy rides — dune adventures at extra cost
Day 3
Merzouga → Dades Valley
Merzouga → Rissani → Todra Gorge → Dades Valley
08:00
Depart
Rissani — Historic Caravan Town
Birthplace of the Alaouite Dynasty · Authentic souk (Sun, Tue & Thu)

After breakfast, depart for the historic town of Rissani. If your tour takes place on Sunday, Tuesday, or Thursday, visit the famous Rissani Traditional Market — one of the oldest and most authentic markets in Morocco. Wander through colourful souks filled with spices, dates, handcrafted goods, and local products while experiencing everyday life in southern Morocco.

11:00
Arrive
Todra Gorge — 300m Limestone Cliffs
Tinghir · 300m sheer cliffs · Crystal-clear Todra River

Continue to Todra Gorge, where towering limestone cliffs rise more than 300 metres above a crystal-clear river cutting through the rock. One of Morocco's most dramatic natural wonders. Enjoy free time to walk through the canyon floor, feel the cool air between the walls, and take memorable photographs of this spectacular landscape.

The Gorge floor walk — the narrowest point is just a few metres wide, with sheer 300m walls rising on both sides
Todra River — a ribbon of icy blue water flowing at the base of the canyon, lined with palm trees and poplar groves
13:30
Lunch
Lunch & Dades Valley
Dramatic rock formations · Palm groves · Berber villages · Night 3

After lunch, travel through the scenic Dades Valley, known for its dramatic rock formations, palm groves, and traditional Berber villages perched on the hillsides. The road winds along the Dades River through a landscape of pink and ochre rock that changes colour as the sun moves. Dinner and overnight stay in a charming hotel overlooking the valley.

Day 4
Dades → Marrakech
Dades Valley → Ouarzazate → Ait Ben Haddou → High Atlas → Marrakech
08:00
Depart
Road of a Thousand Kasbahs → Ouarzazate
Route des Kasbahs · Ouarzazate · "Hollywood of Africa"

After breakfast, continue your journey along the famous Road of a Thousand Kasbahs, lined with fortified mud-brick palaces and ancient towers. Visit Ouarzazate, often called the "Hollywood of Africa" — a major film production hub where Gladiator, Game of Thrones, and Lawrence of Arabia were filmed.

09:30
Visit
Atlas Film Studios — Ouarzazate
World's largest film studio · Movie sets & props from Hollywood blockbusters

Step onto the sets of the world-famous Atlas Film Studios, one of the largest film studios on the planet. Wander through ancient Egyptian temples, Tibetan monasteries, and Roman colosseums built for the big screen, and see props and sets used in Gladiator, The Mummy, Kingdom of Heaven, Prince of Persia, and Game of Thrones.

11:30
Arrive
Ait Ben Haddou — UNESCO World Heritage Site
UNESCO World Heritage · Appeared in Gladiator & Game of Thrones · Free time to explore

Visit the magnificent Kasbah of Ait Ben Haddou — a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of Morocco's most iconic landmarks. This ancient fortified ksar (village) of earthen clay architecture rising above the Ounila River has appeared in dozens of international films and TV series including Gladiator, Game of Thrones, The Mummy, and Kingdom of Heaven. Enjoy free time to explore the ancient fortified village and have lunch.

UNESCO heritage ksar — a living example of southern Moroccan earthen architecture, inhabited for thousands of years
Panoramic viewpoint — climb to the summit granary for a breathtaking view over the entire ksar and valley
14:00
Drive
Tizi n'Tichka Pass — High Atlas Mountains
Altitude 2,260 m · Highest road pass in Morocco · Berber mountain villages

Cross the spectacular High Atlas Mountains via the famous Tizi n'Tichka Pass at 2,260 metres — the highest paved road pass in Morocco. Stop along the way to admire breathtaking mountain scenery, ancient Berber villages clinging to the slopes, and panoramic views stretching to the horizon on both sides of the range.

17:00
Arrive
Arrival in Marrakech — End of Tour
Drop-off at your hotel or riad in Marrakech

Arrive in Marrakech in the late afternoon, where your driver will drop you off at your hotel or riad. Your unforgettable Sahara Desert adventure comes to an end — four days, a continent's worth of landscapes, and memories that last a lifetime.
