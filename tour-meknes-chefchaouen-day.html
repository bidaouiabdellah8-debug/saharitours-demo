About This Tour

Chefchaouen, the famous "Blue Pearl" tucked into the Rif Mountains, is one of Morocco's most photogenic towns — a maze of indigo-washed alleyways, whitewashed rooftops, and mountain air that feels a world away from the imperial cities. Departing directly from Meknes, this full-day round trip drives north through the Rif foothills to spend the day wandering the blue medina, admiring Uta el-Hammam Square and the historic Kasbah, and climbing to the Spanish Mosque for a sweeping panorama over the entire town before returning to Meknes the same evening.

Full-Day Itinerary

07:30
Depart
Departure from Meknes
Meknes → Chefchaouen (≈180 km · ~3.5 hours)

Early pick-up from your hotel or riad in Meknes for the scenic drive north, climbing steadily into the foothills of the Rif Mountains as the landscape turns from plains to pine-dotted hills.

11:00
Explore
The Blue-Painted Medina & Uta el-Hammam Square
Indigo alleyways · Uta el-Hammam Square · Historic Kasbah

Arrive late morning and step straight into Chefchaouen's famous blue medina — every wall, staircase, and doorway washed in shades of indigo and cobalt. Wander freely through the maze of alleyways to Uta el-Hammam Square, the town's lively heart, and visit the historic Kasbah with its small museum and shaded garden.

Blue alleyways — endless shades of indigo painted on walls, steps, and doorframes throughout the old town
Uta el-Hammam Square — the medina's central plaza, lined with cafés and facing the old Kasbah
Historic Kasbah — a 15th-century fortress with a small ethnographic museum and a quiet garden courtyard
13:00
Free Time
Lunch & Shopping
Local restaurants · Handwoven blankets · Leather goods · Goat cheese (lunch not included)

Free time to enjoy lunch at one of the medina's rooftop restaurants, then browse the small shops for handwoven Rif blankets, leatherwork, and the region's traditional local goat cheese.

15:00
Visit
Spanish Mosque Viewpoint
Hilltop panorama over the Blue City · ~45-minute climb

Climb the trail above town to the old Spanish Mosque for the single best view in Chefchaouen — a panorama over the entire blue-washed medina spilling down the hillside, with the Rif Mountains rising behind it.

16:30
Return
Return to Meknes
Chefchaouen → Meknes (≈180 km · ~3.5 hours) · Arrive ~19:30-20:00

Depart the Blue City in the late afternoon and drive back south through the Rif foothills, arriving at your hotel in Meknes in the evening — end of tour.
