About This Tour

Embark on an unforgettable 3-day journey from Fes into the heart of the Moroccan Sahara. Cross the breathtaking Middle Atlas Mountains, pass through cedar forests where wild Barbary macaques roam free, wind through the spectacular Ziz Valley palm oasis, and arrive in Merzouga — gateway to the golden dunes of Erg Chebbi. Ride a camel into the desert at sunset, spend two magical nights under a sky blazing with stars, discover Berber culture through music and traditional food, and explore the ancient caravan town of Rissani before returning to Fes. A journey that stays with you forever.

Short on time or budget? Ask about our 2-Day Express Sahara Tour — the same camel ride and desert camp night, condensed into a quicker round trip from Fes.

3-Day Itinerary

Day 1
Fes → Merzouga
Fes → Ifrane → Cedar Forest → Midelt → Ziz Valley → Merzouga
07:00
Depart
Early Morning Pick-up from Fes
Fes → Middle Atlas Mountains (approx. 1h)

Your adventure begins with an early morning pick-up from your hotel or riad in Fes. Board your comfortable air-conditioned vehicle and head south, climbing gradually into the beautiful Middle Atlas Mountains as the morning light warms the landscape.

08:30
Arrive
Ifrane — "Switzerland of Morocco"
Altitude 1,664 m · Alpine architecture · Clean mountain air

Stop in Ifrane, often called the "Switzerland of Morocco" thanks to its clean streets, alpine-style architecture, and peaceful atmosphere. Stroll around the beautifully manicured parks, visit the famous stone lion, and enjoy the crisp mountain air — a striking contrast to the medina of Fes.

10:00
Drive
Cedar Forest near Azrou — Wild Barbary Macaques
Near Azrou · Ancient cedars · Endemic wildlife

Continue to the famous Cedar Forest near Azrou, where you may see the friendly Barbary macaques in their natural habitat — Morocco's most beloved wild primates, found only here and in Algeria. Walk among ancient cedar trees, some over 800 years old, and breathe the fragrant mountain air.

Wild Barbary macaques — the only wild primates in Africa north of the Sahara, completely habituated to humans
Ancient cedar forest — towering 800-year-old trees in Morocco's largest cedar ecosystem
13:00
Lunch
Lunch in Midelt — "Apple Capital of Morocco"
Midelt · Transition zone between Middle and High Atlas · Local restaurant

After lunch in Midelt, known as the "Apple Capital of Morocco," you begin the dramatic descent into the pre-Saharan south — the landscape transforms from mountain green to rocky red desert plateaus as you approach the Sahara.

15:00
Drive
Ziz Valley — Palm Oasis
Ziz River Valley · Thousands of date palms · Traditional ksour villages

Drive through the spectacular Ziz Valley, home to thousands of palm trees and traditional villages built of red pisé (earthen brick). Stop at the Tunnel du Légionnaire viewpoint for a breathtaking panorama over the palm oasis stretching as far as the eye can see — one of the most dramatic landscapes in Morocco.

Tunnel du Légionnaire viewpoint — a jaw-dropping panorama over the Ziz palm oasis from the rocky plateau
Traditional ksour villages — fortified Berber villages of red earthen brick scattered along the riverbanks
17:30
Arrive
Merzouga — Camel Caravan at Sunset
Erg Chebbi dunes · Up to 150 m high · Camel ride to desert camp

Arrive in Merzouga in the late afternoon, where your camel caravan will be waiting. Ride across the golden dunes of Erg Chebbi while watching one of the most beautiful sunsets in Morocco — the dunes shift colour from amber to deep orange to rose as the sun melts into the horizon. Spend the night in a traditional desert camp with a delicious Moroccan dinner, live Berber music, and a sky full of stars.

Camel caravan into Erg Chebbi — ride into the golden dunes at the magical hour before sunset
Traditional Berber desert camp — private tent, Moroccan dinner, live music around the campfire
Stargazing in the Sahara — no light pollution, millions of stars, one of the world's great skies
Day 2
Explore the Sahara Desert
Merzouga · Erg Chebbi · Nomadic families · Desert culture
06:00
Sunrise
Sahara Sunrise over Erg Chebbi
Dune summit · Golden hour · Desert stillness

Wake up early to witness the breathtaking sunrise over the Sahara. Climb the dune crest before dawn to watch the sky light up in a slow explosion of colour — gold, pink, and orange flooding the horizon as the first rays strike the towering dunes of Erg Chebbi. This is one of the most memorable moments Morocco offers.

09:00
Explore
Explore the Merzouga Region
Nomadic families · Khamlia village · Optional ATV / buggy rides

After breakfast, explore the Merzouga region at leisure. Visit nomadic Tuareg and Berber families living on the edge of the dunes, discover traditional desert life and crafts, and enjoy panoramic views of the endless Sahara. Visit Khamlia village to experience traditional Gnawa music — a hypnotic ancestral rhythm unique to the deep south of Morocco. Optional activities include ATV or buggy rides across the dunes (extra cost).

Nomadic Berber family visit — traditional desert hospitality, mint tea, and an authentic glimpse into Saharan life
Khamlia — Gnawa music village — ancient spiritual music performed by the descendants of West African slaves
Optional: ATV / quad or buggy ride — thrilling dune adventures available at extra cost
19:00
Night 2
Second Night in the Desert
Desert camp or hotel near the dunes · Choice available

Spend another memorable night in the desert camp or at a comfortable hotel near the dunes — your choice. Dinner under the stars, more Berber music around the campfire, and the profound silence of the Sahara at night.

Day 3
Merzouga → Fes
Merzouga → Rissani → Ziz Valley → Midelt → Ifrane → Fes
06:00
Sunrise
Final Sahara Sunrise

Wake up early to witness the magical sunrise over the golden dunes of Erg Chebbi one last time. After breakfast at the desert camp, pack up and prepare for the journey back north to Fes.

08:30
Arrive
Rissani — Ancient Caravan Capital
Historical Tafilalt capital · Alaouite Dynasty · Authentic souk

Depart for the historic town of Rissani, the ancient capital of the Tafilalt region and one of Morocco's most authentic desert towns — birthplace of the Alaouite Dynasty, the royal family that has ruled Morocco since 1666. Explore the traditional Rissani souk, where locals trade spices, dates, handicrafts, and livestock. Discover the rich history, browse the lively souks, and experience the genuine atmosphere of deep southern Morocco — far from the tourist trail.

Rissani souk — one of Morocco's most authentic markets, especially on Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Sundays
Alaouite history — the birthplace of the dynasty that still rules Morocco today, with ancient kasbahs and mausoleums
10:30
Drive
Return through the Ziz Valley
Ziz Valley panoramic views · Palm oasis · Return through Atlas

Continue your journey through the spectacular Ziz Valley, famous for its breathtaking panoramic views and thousands of palm trees stretching across the oasis. The late-morning light gives the valley a different quality from the day before — a parting gift of beauty from the desert.

13:00
Lunch
Lunch in Midelt

Stop for lunch in Midelt, known as the "Apple Capital of Morocco," before crossing the beautiful Middle Atlas Mountains on the return journey north.

15:30
Stop
Short Break in Ifrane

Enjoy a short break in Ifrane, often called the "Switzerland of Morocco" for its clean streets, European-style architecture, and peaceful surroundings. Stretch your legs in the mountain air before the final drive back to Fes.

17:30
Arrive
Arrive in Fes — End of Tour
Drop-off at your hotel or riad in Fes

Arrive in Fes in the late afternoon, where your driver will drop you off at your hotel or riad, marking the end of your unforgettable Sahara Desert adventure. Three days, two contrasting worlds — from medina to Sahara and back — carrying memories that last a lifetime.
