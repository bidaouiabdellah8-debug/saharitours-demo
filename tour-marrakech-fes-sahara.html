About This Tour

Begin your Moroccan adventure in Marrakech and travel overland across the High Atlas Mountains, the ancient kasbahs of the south, and the golden dunes of the Sahara Desert before arriving in the imperial city of Fes. This 4-day journey is the mirror image of our most popular Sahara crossing — ideal for travelers flying into Marrakech and out of Fes, or simply wanting to experience Morocco's greatest landmarks in this order. Expect UNESCO heritage sites, unforgettable desert camps, and mountain passes that rank among the most scenic drives on Earth.

Flying in and out of the same city instead? Our Marrakech Desert Loop covers the same Sahara highlights as a round trip — or take this exact route the other way with our Fes to Marrakech via Sahara tour.

4-Day Itinerary

Day 1
Marrakech → Dades Valley
Marrakech → Tizi n'Tichka → Ait Ben Haddou → Ouarzazate → Dades Valley
07:00
Depart
Early Morning Pick-up from Marrakech

Your adventure begins with an early morning pick-up from your hotel or riad in Marrakech. Head south-east out of the city and begin the steady climb into the High Atlas Mountains, Morocco's great mountain spine separating the fertile north from the pre-Saharan south.

09:30
Cross
Tizi n'Tichka Pass — High Atlas Mountains
Altitude 2,260 m · Morocco's highest paved road pass · Berber mountain villages

Cross the spectacular High Atlas via the legendary Tizi n'Tichka Pass, the highest paved road in the country. Stop along the winding switchbacks to admire sweeping mountain views, ancient Berber villages clinging to the slopes, and roadside stalls selling local minerals and geodes.

11:30
Arrive
Ait Ben Haddou — UNESCO World Heritage Site
UNESCO World Heritage · Gladiator & Game of Thrones filming location · Free time & lunch

Arrive at the magnificent Kasbah of Ait Ben Haddou — a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of Morocco's most iconic landmarks. This fortified ksar of earthen clay architecture rising above the Ounila River has appeared in dozens of international films and TV series, including Gladiator and Game of Thrones. Enjoy free time to explore the ancient village and have lunch.

UNESCO heritage ksar — a living example of southern Moroccan earthen architecture, inhabited for thousands of years
Panoramic viewpoint — climb to the summit granary for a breathtaking view over the ksar and valley
14:00
Visit
Ouarzazate — "Hollywood of Africa" & Atlas Film Studios
World's largest film studio · Movie sets & props from Hollywood blockbusters

Continue to Ouarzazate, often called the "Hollywood of Africa" — a major film production hub where Gladiator, Game of Thrones, and Lawrence of Arabia were filmed. Step onto the sets of the world-famous Atlas Film Studios (optional visit), wandering through ancient Egyptian temples and Roman colosseums built for the big screen.

16:00
Arrive
Road of a Thousand Kasbahs → Dades Valley
Route des Kasbahs · Fortified mud-brick palaces · Red-rock landscapes · Night 1

Travel the famous Road of a Thousand Kasbahs, lined with fortified mud-brick palaces and ancient towers, into the Dades Valley — known for its dramatic red-rock landscapes and traditional Berber villages perched on the hillsides. Dinner and overnight stay in a charming hotel overlooking the valley.

Day 2
Dades → Merzouga
Dades Valley → Todra Gorge → Tinghir → Rissani → Merzouga
08:00
Depart
Dades Valley — Morning Departure

After breakfast, depart through the dramatic Dades Valley, where the road winds along the Dades River through a landscape of pink and ochre rock. Enjoy the changing colours of the gorge walls as the morning light moves across the cliffs.

10:00
Arrive
Todra Gorge — 300m Limestone Cliffs
Tinghir · 300m sheer cliffs · Crystal-clear Todra River

Arrive at Todra Gorge, where towering limestone cliffs rise more than 300 metres above a crystal-clear river cutting through the rock — one of Morocco's most dramatic natural wonders. Enjoy free time to walk the canyon floor and photograph the spectacular scenery.

The gorge floor walk — the narrowest point is just a few metres wide, with sheer 300m walls rising on both sides
Todra River — a ribbon of icy blue water flowing at the base of the canyon, lined with palm trees and poplar groves
12:30
Continue
Tinghir & Rissani — Historic Caravan Town
Birthplace of the Alaouite Dynasty · Authentic souk (Sun, Tue & Thu)

Continue south through Tinghir toward Rissani, the historic caravan town and birthplace of the Alaouite Dynasty. If your tour takes place on Sunday, Tuesday, or Thursday, visit the famous Rissani Traditional Market — one of the oldest and most authentic markets in Morocco, filled with spices, dates, and handcrafted goods.

16:30
Arrive
Merzouga — Camel Ride & Desert Camp
Erg Chebbi dunes · Up to 150m high · Night 2 in desert camp

Arrive in Merzouga during the late afternoon. Meet your camel caravan and ride across the magnificent Erg Chebbi dunes while enjoying an unforgettable desert sunset. Spend the night in a traditional or luxury desert camp with dinner, live Berber music, and stargazing under one of the world's darkest skies.

Camel trekking into Erg Chebbi at golden sunset — included in price
Desert camp — traditional Berber tent, Moroccan dinner, live music around the campfire
Day 3
Explore the Sahara
Merzouga · Sunrise · Khamlia · Desert Culture
06:00
Sunrise
Sahara Sunrise over Erg Chebbi

Wake up early to admire one of the most beautiful sunrises in Morocco — the dunes shifting from deep purple to amber gold as the first light floods the horizon. Return from camp for breakfast and discover the wonders of the Merzouga region.

09:00
Explore
Khamlia Village, Nomadic Families & Desert Activities
Khamlia · Nomadic families · Fossil areas · Night 2 in hotel

Visit Khamlia Village, famous for its traditional Gnawa music. Meet local nomadic families and learn about their desert lifestyle and traditions. Explore the fossil areas around Merzouga and enjoy panoramic views of the Sahara. In the afternoon, relax at your hotel near the dunes or choose optional activities such as ATV or buggy rides across the dunes.

Khamlia Gnawa music — ancient ancestral rhythms unique to the deep south of Morocco
Optional ATV / buggy rides — dune adventures at extra cost
Day 4
Merzouga → Fes
Merzouga → Ziz Valley → Midelt → Ifrane → Fes
08:00
Depart
Ziz Valley — Palm Oasis & Gorge
Pre-Saharan palm oasis · Traditional ksour villages · Dramatic gorge

After breakfast, begin the journey north through the spectacular Ziz Valley, one of Morocco's largest palm oases, with thousands of date palms stretching across the pre-Saharan landscape and traditional ksour villages carved from red earth.

11:00
Lunch
Lunch in Midelt

Stop for lunch in Midelt — the "Apple Capital of Morocco" — a Middle Atlas mountain town at the foot of the Ayachi massif, before continuing north toward the cedar forests.

13:00
Stop
Cedar Forest — Barbary Macaques
Near Azrou · Ancient cedar ecosystem · Endemic wildlife

Continue to the Cedar Forest near Azrou, where you may encounter the famous Barbary macaques — Morocco's beloved wild primates — roaming freely through ancient trees some over 800 years old.

15:00
Stop
Ifrane — "Switzerland of Morocco"
Alpine-style architecture · 1,664m altitude · Peaceful mountain town

Arrive in Ifrane, nicknamed the "Switzerland of Morocco" for its European-style architecture, sloped rooftops, and peaceful atmosphere at 1,664m altitude. Enjoy a short stop to stretch your legs and take photos in this unusual corner of Morocco.

17:00
Arrive
Arrival in Fes — End of Tour
Drop-off at your hotel or riad in Fes

Arrive in Fes in the late afternoon or early evening, where your driver will drop you off at your hotel or riad. Your unforgettable journey from Marrakech to Fes across the Sahara comes to an end — four days, a continent's worth of landscapes, and memories that last a lifetime.
