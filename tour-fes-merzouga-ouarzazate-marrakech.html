About This Tour

Experience the best of southern Morocco in just four unforgettable days. Travel from the cultural city of Fes through the Middle Atlas Mountains, the spectacular Sahara Desert of Merzouga, the dramatic landscapes of the Dades Valley and Ouarzazate, and finish your journey in the vibrant city of Marrakech. This private desert journey combines breathtaking landscapes, authentic Berber culture, ancient kasbahs, desert adventures, and unforgettable moments in the Moroccan Sahara.

A Journey Through the Real Morocco — this tour is built for travelers who want two full days and two nights immersed in the Sahara rather than rushing through it: a camel trek and desert camp on night one, then an entire second day exploring the dunes by 4x4, visiting the Gnawa music village of Khamlia, and an optional quad ride, before continuing to Ouarzazate, Ait Ben Haddou and Marrakech. Want a full day of Marrakech sightseeing instead? See our Fes to Marrakech via Sahara + Marrakech City Day tour, or for the shortest classic route, our Classic 4-Day Fes to Marrakech via Sahara tour.

4-Day Itinerary

Day 1
Fes → Merzouga
Fes → Ifrane → Azrou → Midelt → Ziz Valley → Erfoud → Merzouga
07:00
Depart
Early Morning Departure from Fes
Ifrane · 1,664m altitude · Alpine mountain town

Your journey begins with an early morning pick-up from your hotel or riad in Fes. Head into the Middle Atlas Mountains and stop in Ifrane, admired for its European-style architecture, tidy streets and cool mountain scenery at 1,664m altitude.

09:30
Stop
Azrou & the Cedar Forest — Barbary Macaques
Near Azrou · Ancient cedar forest · Possible Barbary macaques

Continue to Azrou and the Cedar Forest, with a possible stop to spot the famous wild Barbary macaques among the centuries-old trees, before carrying on through classic Middle Atlas mountain scenery.

13:00
Lunch
Lunch Break in Midelt

Stop for a well-earned lunch break in Midelt, known for its mountain landscapes and apple production, nestled between the Middle and High Atlas ranges.

14:30
View
Ziz Valley & Ziz Gorge
Panoramic viewpoints · Palm oasis · Dramatic gorge scenery

In the afternoon, enjoy spectacular views over the Ziz Valley and the Ziz Gorge, one of Morocco's largest palm oases, with thousands of date palms and traditional ksour villages stretching across the pre-Saharan landscape.

16:00
Pass
Errachidia & Erfoud
Errachidia · Erfoud — fossils & dates

Continue through Errachidia and on to Erfoud, known throughout Morocco for its fossils and date palms, before the final stretch toward the dunes.

17:30
Arrive
Merzouga — Camel Ride to the Dunes & Desert Camp
Erg Chebbi dunes · Sunset camel trek · Night 1 in desert camp

Arrive in Merzouga in the late afternoon. In the evening, transfer by camel out into the Erg Chebbi dunes to watch the sunset paint the Sahara gold, then continue to your desert camp for a traditional dinner, live Berber music and stargazing under some of the darkest skies on Earth.

Camel trek into Erg Chebbi at golden sunset — included in price
Desert camp — traditional Berber tent, Moroccan dinner & breakfast included
Day 2
Merzouga Desert Day
A Full Day Exploring the Merzouga Desert
06:00
Sunrise
Sunrise over the Erg Chebbi Dunes

Wake early for sunrise over the Erg Chebbi dunes, watching the sand shift from deep purple to amber gold. Enjoy breakfast at camp — unlike a one-night desert stop, today there's no rush to pack up and drive on.

09:00
Explore
4x4 Desert Excursion Around Merzouga
Local 4x4 excursion · Unique desert landscapes · Nomad families

After breakfast, set out on a local 4x4 excursion exploring the Merzouga region in depth — unique desert landscapes, wide-open erg scenery, and the chance to meet local nomad and Berber communities to learn about traditional desert life.

Off-road 4x4 tour — a full morning exploring dunes and desert tracks most visitors never see
Nomad & Berber families — an authentic look at traditional desert life
11:00
Visit
Khamlia — Home of Gnawa Music
Khamlia village · Traditional Gnawa music & culture

Continue to Khamlia, a small desert village famous throughout Morocco for keeping the traditional Gnawa music alive — descended from West African communities, with hypnotic rhythms played on metal castanets and the guembri lute.

13:00
Lunch
Lunch in the Merzouga Area

Stop for lunch back in the Merzouga area before an easy afternoon of further desert discovery and rest.

14:30
Discover
Seasonal Lake & Desert Oases
Season-dependent · Dayet Srji seasonal lake · Palm oases

Depending on the season and recent rainfall, visit the Merzouga seasonal lake, a surprising mirror of water and pink flamingos in the middle of the dunes, along with nearby palm oases and panoramic Sahara viewpoints.

16:00
Free Time
Free Afternoon — Relax or Optional Quad Biking
Free time · Walk the dunes · Optional quad biking (paid locally)

Return to your accommodation in the late afternoon with free time to relax, walk the dunes at your own pace, or add an optional quad biking excursion into the Erg Chebbi at your own expense.

Evening
Overnight
Second Night in the Merzouga Area
Night 2 · Merzouga area · Comfort hotel or riad

Dinner and a second overnight stay in the Merzouga area, arranged to your preferred comfort level — from a comfortable local hotel or riad back to the traditional desert camp, if you'd rather sleep under the stars a second time.

Day 3
Merzouga → Ouarzazate
Merzouga → Todra Gorges → Dades Valley → Rose Valley → Skoura → Ouarzazate
08:00
Depart
Leaving the Sahara — Toward Tinghir

After breakfast, leave the Sahara behind and head west via Rissani and Erfoud toward Tinghir, watching the landscape shift from desert flats into the foothills of the Atlas.

11:00
Arrive
Todra Gorges — 300m Limestone Cliffs
Tinghir · 300m sheer cliffs · Todra Valley palm groves

Arrive at the towering Todra Gorges, where limestone cliffs rise over 300 metres above a crystal-clear river. Walk the canyon floor between the sheer rock walls, then pass back through the palm groves of the Todra Valley.

13:00
Lunch
Lunch near Tinghir

Stop for lunch in the Tinghir / Todra area before continuing the drive south-west toward the Dades Valley.

14:30
Drive
Dades Valley & Rose Valley
Red-rock landscapes · Kalaa M'Gouna rose fields · Winding roads

Drive through the scenic Dades Valley, known for its red-rock landscapes, traditional villages and winding roads, then continue through the Rose Valley around Kalaa M'Gouna — especially beautiful in flowering season — famous for its rose plantations and rosewater production.

16:00
Visit
Skoura Oasis
Palm-filled oasis · Numerous traditional kasbahs

Continue into the palm-filled Skoura oasis, dotted with numerous traditional earthen kasbahs beneath thousands of palms, before the final stretch toward Ouarzazate.

18:00
Arrive
Arrival in Ouarzazate
"Gateway to the Sahara" · Night 3 in hotel

Arrive in Ouarzazate, the "Gateway to the Sahara," known for its cinematic history. Time permitting, visit the Taourirt Kasbah or the Atlas Film Studios before dinner and an overnight stay in a comfortable hotel.

Day 4
Ouarzazate → Marrakech
Ouarzazate → Ait Ben Haddou → High Atlas Mountains → Marrakech
08:30
Visit
Ait Ben Haddou — UNESCO World Heritage Kasbah
UNESCO World Heritage · Iconic international film location

After breakfast, head to one of Morocco's most famous fortified villages — Ait Ben Haddou, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Explore its narrow earthen streets and climb toward the panoramic viewpoint above the ksar and valley.

UNESCO heritage ksar — a living example of southern Moroccan earthen architecture
Panoramic viewpoint — climb to the summit granary for a breathtaking view over the ksar and valley
11:00
Optional
Telouet Kasbah (Optional, Depending on Time)
Former Glaoui palace · Ornate painted interiors

Time and road conditions permitting, make a short detour to Telouet, the crumbling former palace of the Glaoui pashas, with faded but still striking painted ceilings and carved plasterwork.

13:00
Drive
Tizi n'Tichka Pass — High Atlas Mountains
Altitude 2,260 m · Highest road pass in Morocco · Berber villages

Cross the spectacular High Atlas Mountains via the famous Tizi n'Tichka Pass at 2,260 metres — the highest paved road pass in Morocco. Stop along the way for photos of the mountain scenery and Berber villages clinging to the slopes.

17:00
End
Arrival in Marrakech — Tour Ends
Drop-off at your hotel or riad, or airport transfer on request

Continue to Marrakech, arriving in the afternoon or evening depending on road and traffic conditions. Your driver drops you off at your hotel or riad (or transfers you to the airport if that's your onward plan). There's no extra sightseeing day built in — your four-day journey through the mountains and two full days of Sahara ends here, in the Red City.
