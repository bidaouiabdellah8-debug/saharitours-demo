About This Tour

This is the relaxed, deluxe version of our best-selling Fes to Marrakech desert crossing — the same unforgettable landscapes, but spread across five unhurried days and four nights instead of four. Cross the Middle Atlas Mountains to the golden dunes of the Sahara, spend a full day exploring Merzouga at your own pace, then wind through Todra Gorge, the Dades Valley, and two extra named stops the shorter versions skip entirely — the fragrant Rose Valley of Kalaa M'Gouna and the palm-filled Skoura Oasis — before Ait Ben Haddou, the High Atlas, and Marrakech. More time to breathe, more places to stop, and an extra overnight for travelers who don't want to rush.

Short on time or budget? Our 3-Day Express covers the same crossing in a faster, more affordable version. Prefer the classic pace? See our Classic 4-Day Fes to Marrakech via Sahara tour, or add a full sightseeing day in the Red City with our 4-Day Fes to Marrakech via Sahara + Marrakech City Day.

5-Day Itinerary

Day 1
Fes → Merzouga
Fes → Ifrane → Midelt → Ziz Valley → Merzouga
07:00
Depart
Early Morning Pick-up from Fes — Ifrane
Ifrane · 1,664 m altitude · Alpine mountain town

Your relaxed five-day journey begins with an unhurried morning pick-up from your hotel or riad in Fes. Head into the scenic Middle Atlas Mountains and stop in Ifrane, admired for its European-style alpine architecture and cool mountain air at 1,664m altitude.

09:30
Stop
Cedar Forest near Azrou — Barbary Macaques
Near Azrou · Ancient cedar ecosystem · Endemic wildlife

Continue at an easy pace to the Cedar Forest near Azrou, where you may encounter the famous Barbary macaques roaming freely among trees some over 800 years old — a chance to stretch your legs and enjoy the mountain scenery without feeling rushed.

13:00
Lunch
Lunch in Midelt

A relaxed lunch stop in Midelt — the "Apple Capital of Morocco" — nestled between the Middle and High Atlas ranges, before the afternoon drive toward the pre-Saharan valleys.

14:30
View
Ziz Valley & Gorge
Panoramic viewpoints · Palm oasis · Dramatic gorge scenery

Drive through the dramatic Ziz Valley and Ziz Gorge, one of Morocco's largest palm oases, with thousands of date palms and traditional ksour villages carved from red earth stretching across the pre-Saharan landscape.

17:00
Arrive
Merzouga — Camel Ride & Desert Camp (Night 1)
Erg Chebbi dunes · Sunset camel trek · Night 1 in desert camp

Arrive in Merzouga during the late afternoon. In the evening, meet your camel caravan for a ride into the Erg Chebbi dunes to watch the sunset paint the Sahara gold, then continue to your desert camp for a traditional dinner, live Berber music around the campfire, and stargazing under some of the darkest skies on Earth.

Camel trek into Erg Chebbi at golden sunset — included in price
Desert camp — traditional Berber tent, Moroccan dinner, live music around the campfire
Day 2
Merzouga Desert Experience
A Full Day Exploring the Merzouga Desert
06:00
Sunrise
Sahara Sunrise over Erg Chebbi

Wake up early to admire one of the most beautiful sunrises in Morocco — the dunes shifting from deep purple to amber gold as the first light floods the horizon. Return from camp and enjoy a leisurely breakfast, with a full day ahead dedicated entirely to the desert.

09:30
Explore
Khamlia Village, Nomadic Families & Fossil Areas
Khamlia · Nomadic families · Fossil areas · Gnawa music

With no need to pack up and drive on, spend the morning visiting Khamlia village, famous for its traditional Gnawa music. Meet local nomadic families, learn about their desert lifestyle and traditions, and explore the fossil-rich areas surrounding Merzouga at a relaxed, unhurried pace.

Khamlia Gnawa music — ancient ancestral rhythms unique to the deep south of Morocco
Optional quad biking — dune adventures at extra cost
15:00
Free Time
Free Afternoon Near the Dunes (Night 2)
Hotel near the dunes · Free time to relax · Night 2 in Merzouga area

In the afternoon, relax at your hotel near the dunes with genuine free time — read, swim, or simply watch the light change over Erg Chebbi. Second night in the Merzouga area, in a comfortable hotel rather than another rushed transfer.

Day 3
Merzouga → Dades Valley
Merzouga → Rissani → Todra Gorge → Dades Valley
08:00
Depart
Rissani — Historic Caravan Town
Birthplace of the Alaouite Dynasty · Authentic souk (Sun, Tue & Thu)

After a relaxed breakfast, depart for the historic town of Rissani. If your tour falls on a Sunday, Tuesday, or Thursday, visit the famous Rissani Traditional Market — one of the oldest and most authentic markets in Morocco.

11:00
Arrive
Todra Gorge — 300m Limestone Cliffs
Tinghir · 300m sheer cliffs · Crystal-clear Todra River

Continue to Todra Gorge, where towering limestone cliffs rise more than 300 metres above a crystal-clear river. With a full itinerary and no need to rush, enjoy generous free time to walk the canyon floor, feel the cool air between the walls, and take memorable photographs.

The Gorge floor walk — the narrowest point is just a few metres wide, with sheer 300m walls rising on both sides
Todra River — a ribbon of icy blue water flowing at the base of the canyon, lined with palm trees and poplar groves
13:30
Lunch
Lunch near Tinghir

A relaxed lunch stop in the Tinghir / Todra area before continuing south-west into the Dades Valley.

15:00
Arrive
Dades Valley — Overnight (Night 3)
Dramatic rock formations · Palm groves · Berber villages · Night 3

Travel at a comfortable pace through the scenic Dades Valley, known for its dramatic red-rock formations, palm groves, and traditional Berber villages perched on the hillsides, winding along the Dades River. Dinner and overnight stay in a charming hotel or kasbah overlooking the valley.

Day 4
Dades → Ouarzazate
Dades Valley → Rose Valley → Skoura Oasis → Ouarzazate
08:30
Depart
Road of a Thousand Kasbahs
Route des Kasbahs · Fortified mud-brick palaces · Ancient towers

After a leisurely breakfast, continue along the famous Road of a Thousand Kasbahs, lined with fortified mud-brick palaces and ancient towers — one of the highlights unique to this more relaxed itinerary, with time to actually stop and look.

10:30
Visit
Rose Valley — Kalaa M'Gouna
Kalaa M'Gouna · Rose plantations · Rosewater production

Stop in the Rose Valley around Kalaa M'Gouna — especially beautiful in flowering season — famous for its rose plantations and traditional rosewater production. A stop the shorter 3 and 4-day versions of this route simply don't have time for.

12:30
Visit
Skoura Oasis
Palm-filled oasis · Numerous traditional kasbahs

Continue into the palm-filled Skoura Oasis, home to numerous traditional earthen kasbahs beneath thousands of date palms — a second exclusive stop that gives this relaxed version its distinct, unhurried character.

14:30
Arrive
Ouarzazate — Atlas Film Studios & Taourirt Kasbah (Night 4)
"Hollywood of Africa" · World's largest film studio · Night 4 in hotel

Arrive in Ouarzazate, often called the "Hollywood of Africa." Visit the Atlas Film Studios, one of the largest film studios in the world, and/or the historic Taourirt Kasbah, a former Glaoui stronghold with ornate painted interiors. Dinner and overnight stay in Ouarzazate — the fourth and final night before Marrakech.

Atlas Film Studios — sets and props from Gladiator, The Mummy, and Game of Thrones
Taourirt Kasbah — a former Glaoui stronghold with ornate painted interiors
Day 5
Ouarzazate → Marrakech
Ouarzazate → Ait Ben Haddou → High Atlas → Marrakech
08:30
Visit
Ait Ben Haddou — UNESCO World Heritage Ksar
UNESCO World Heritage · Gladiator & Game of Thrones filming location · Free time to explore

After breakfast, visit the magnificent Kasbah of Ait Ben Haddou — a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of Morocco's most iconic landmarks. This ancient fortified ksar of earthen clay architecture has appeared in Gladiator, Game of Thrones, and many other productions. With a relaxed schedule, enjoy generous free time to explore the fortified village and climb to the panoramic viewpoint.

UNESCO heritage ksar — a living example of southern Moroccan earthen architecture, inhabited for thousands of years
Panoramic viewpoint — climb to the summit granary for a breathtaking view over the entire ksar and valley
12:00
Drive
Tizi n'Tichka Pass — High Atlas Mountains
Altitude 2,260 m · Highest road pass in Morocco · Berber mountain villages

Cross the spectacular High Atlas Mountains via the famous Tizi n'Tichka Pass at 2,260 metres — the highest paved road pass in Morocco. Stop along the way to admire breathtaking mountain scenery and ancient Berber villages clinging to the slopes, with plenty of time for photos.

17:00
Arrive
Arrival in Marrakech — End of Tour
Drop-off at your hotel or riad in Marrakech

Arrive in Marrakech in the afternoon or evening, where your driver will drop you off at your hotel or riad. Your relaxed five-day Sahara adventure comes to an end — an extra day, an extra night, and two extra stops that let you actually experience Morocco instead of racing through it.
