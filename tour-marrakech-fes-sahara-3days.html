About This Tour

This is the fast, efficient way to cross Morocco from Marrakech to Fes via the Sahara — the same headline highlights as our classic 4-day route, condensed into 3 days and 2 nights instead of 4. You still get the High Atlas and the Tizi n'Tichka Pass, the UNESCO ksar of Ait Ben Haddou, Ouarzazate's Atlas Film Studios, the Dades Valley, the towering Todra Gorge, a sunset camel trek into the Erg Chebbi dunes, a night under the stars in a traditional desert camp, the Ziz Valley palm oasis, and Ifrane — just with one night in the desert instead of two, and a faster overall pace. Ideal for travelers flying into Marrakech and out of Fes who are short on time or budget but don't want to miss a single highlight.

Got an extra day to spare? Our classic 4-Day Marrakech to Fes via Sahara tour adds a full second day exploring the Merzouga region, or take this exact route the other way with our 3-Day Fes to Marrakech Express tour.

3-Day Express Itinerary

Day 1
Marrakech → Todra Gorge
Marrakech → Tizi n'Tichka → Ait Ben Haddou → Ouarzazate → Dades Valley → Tinghir
07:00
Depart
Early Morning Pick-up from Marrakech

Your express Sahara adventure begins with an early morning pick-up from your hotel or riad in Marrakech. Head south-east out of the city and begin the steady climb into the High Atlas Mountains, Morocco's great mountain spine separating the fertile north from the pre-Saharan south.

09:30
Cross
Tizi n'Tichka Pass — High Atlas Mountains
Altitude 2,260 m · Morocco's highest paved road pass · Berber mountain villages

Cross the spectacular High Atlas via the legendary Tizi n'Tichka Pass, the highest paved road in the country. Stop along the winding switchbacks to admire sweeping mountain views, ancient Berber villages clinging to the slopes, and roadside stalls selling local minerals and geodes.

11:30
Arrive
Ait Ben Haddou — UNESCO World Heritage Site
UNESCO World Heritage · Gladiator & Game of Thrones filming location · Free time & lunch

Arrive at the magnificent Kasbah of Ait Ben Haddou — a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of Morocco's most iconic landmarks. This fortified ksar of earthen clay architecture rising above the Ounila River has appeared in dozens of international films and TV series, including Gladiator and Game of Thrones. Enjoy free time to explore the ancient village and have lunch — the express pace keeps this stop efficient but unhurried.

UNESCO heritage ksar — a living example of southern Moroccan earthen architecture, inhabited for thousands of years
Panoramic viewpoint — climb to the summit granary for a breathtaking view over the ksar and valley
14:00
Visit
Ouarzazate — "Hollywood of Africa" & Atlas Film Studios
World's largest film studio · Movie sets & props from Hollywood blockbusters

Continue to Ouarzazate, often called the "Hollywood of Africa" — a major film production hub where Gladiator, Game of Thrones, and Lawrence of Arabia were filmed. Step onto the sets of the world-famous Atlas Film Studios (optional visit), wandering through ancient Egyptian temples and Roman colosseums built for the big screen.

16:30
Arrive
Road of a Thousand Kasbahs → Dades Valley & Tinghir
Route des Kasbahs · Fortified mud-brick palaces · Red-rock landscapes · Night 1 near Todra Gorge

Travel the famous Road of a Thousand Kasbahs, lined with fortified mud-brick palaces and ancient towers, through the Dades Valley and its dramatic red-rock landscapes. In this express version the drive continues on toward Tinghir, at the gateway to the Todra Gorge — covering more ground than the classic 4-day tour's first day, so tomorrow morning starts right at the gorge. Dinner and overnight in the Todra/Tinghir area.

Day 2
Todra Gorge → Merzouga
Todra Gorge → Tinghir → Rissani → Merzouga (Desert Camp)
08:00
Explore
Todra Gorge — 300m Limestone Cliffs
Tinghir · 300m sheer cliffs · Crystal-clear Todra River

After breakfast, head straight to the towering Todra Gorge, where limestone cliffs rise more than 300 metres above a crystal-clear river cutting through the rock — one of Morocco's most dramatic natural wonders. Enjoy a walk along the canyon floor and photograph the spectacular scenery before pressing on toward the desert.

The gorge floor walk — the narrowest point is just a few metres wide, with sheer 300m walls rising on both sides
Todra River — a ribbon of icy blue water flowing at the base of the canyon, lined with palm trees and poplar groves
11:00
Continue
Tinghir & Rissani — Historic Caravan Town
Birthplace of the Alaouite Dynasty · Authentic souk (Sun, Tue & Thu)

Continue south through Tinghir toward Rissani, the historic caravan town and birthplace of the Alaouite Dynasty. If your tour takes place on Sunday, Tuesday, or Thursday, visit the famous Rissani Traditional Market — one of the oldest and most authentic markets in Morocco, filled with spices, dates, and handcrafted goods.

16:30
Arrive
Merzouga — Camel Ride & Desert Camp
Erg Chebbi dunes · Up to 150m high · Night in desert camp

Arrive in Merzouga during the late afternoon. Meet your camel caravan and ride across the magnificent Erg Chebbi dunes while enjoying an unforgettable desert sunset. Spend the night in a traditional desert camp with dinner, live Berber music, and stargazing under one of the world's darkest skies — a single, unforgettable night in the dunes rather than two, keeping the express pace.

Camel trekking into Erg Chebbi at golden sunset — included in price
Desert camp — traditional Berber tent, Moroccan dinner, live music around the campfire
Day 3
Merzouga → Fes
Merzouga → Ziz Valley → Midelt → Ifrane → Fes
06:00
Sunrise
Sahara Sunrise & Departure from Camp

Wake up early to admire one of the most beautiful sunrises in Morocco — the dunes shifting from deep purple to amber gold as the first light floods the horizon. After breakfast at camp, depart straight from Merzouga — the express itinerary skips the second desert day entirely, so the whole final leg to Fes happens today.

08:00
Depart
Ziz Valley — Palm Oasis & Gorge
Pre-Saharan palm oasis · Traditional ksour villages · Dramatic gorge

Begin the journey north through the spectacular Ziz Valley, one of Morocco's largest palm oases, with thousands of date palms stretching across the pre-Saharan landscape and traditional ksour villages carved from red earth.

11:00
Lunch
Lunch in Midelt

Stop for lunch in Midelt — the "Apple Capital of Morocco" — a Middle Atlas mountain town at the foot of the Ayachi massif, before continuing north toward the cedar forests.

13:00
Stop
Cedar Forest — Barbary Macaques
Near Azrou · Ancient cedar ecosystem · Endemic wildlife

Continue to the Cedar Forest near Azrou, where you may encounter the famous Barbary macaques — Morocco's beloved wild primates — roaming freely through ancient trees some over 800 years old.

15:00
Stop
Ifrane — "Switzerland of Morocco"
Alpine-style architecture · 1,664m altitude · Peaceful mountain town

Arrive in Ifrane, nicknamed the "Switzerland of Morocco" for its European-style architecture, sloped rooftops, and peaceful atmosphere at 1,664m altitude. Enjoy a short stop to stretch your legs and take photos in this unusual corner of Morocco.

17:00
Arrive
Arrival in Fes — End of Tour
Drop-off at your hotel or riad in Fes

Arrive in Fes in the late afternoon or early evening, where your driver will drop you off at your hotel or riad. Three days, one night in the desert, and every headline highlight of the Marrakech-to-Fes Sahara crossing — the fast way to see it all.
